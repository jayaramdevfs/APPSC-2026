### 1. With reference to deepfakes, consider the following statements:
1. They are manipulated digital media created using generative artificial intelligence.
2. Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) are the primary algorithms used in their creation.
3. In India, deepfakes are currently governed by a specific, dedicated legislation called the "Deepfake Prevention Act 2024".

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2, and 3

**Answer:** (a)
**Explanation:** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Deepfakes use AI and GANs to create highly realistic but false media. Statement 3 is incorrect; India does not yet have a dedicated "Deepfake Prevention Act", and such issues are currently addressed under the IT Act 2000 and the Indian Copyright Act 1957.

### 9. Why is "Guar Gum" (Cluster Bean) significant in the extraction of shale gas?

### 11. "Net Energy Gain", a term frequently used in the context of nuclear fusion, refers to:
(a) The difference between the energy produced by a reactor and the energy consumed by the national grid.
(b) A condition where the fusion reaction produces more energy than it consumes to sustain itself.
(c) The total energy efficiency of a nuclear power plant including transmission losses.
(d) The process of recycling spent nuclear fuel for civilian use.

**Answer:** (b)

**Topic:** Nuclear Fusion

### 15. The "Aditya-L1" mission of ISRO is placed at the Lagrangian Point L1 because:
(a) It is the point closest to the Sun in Earth's orbit.
(b) It allows the satellite to view the Sun continuously without any occultation or eclipses.
(c) It is the location where the Moon's gravity cancels out the Sun's gravity.
