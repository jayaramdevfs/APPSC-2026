## I. Digital Platforms & E-Governance
Modern governance leverages emerging technologies like Artificial Intelligence (AI), Blockchain, and Quantum Computing to improve service delivery and transparency.

### 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Regulation
*   **Bletchley Declaration (2023):** The world's first global agreement on AI safety, signed by 28 countries (including India) and the EU. It focuses on identifying and managing risks from "Frontier AI" (highly capable general-purpose models).
*   **Deepfakes:** Manipulated digital media (video/audio) using deep learning algorithms (specifically **Generative Adversarial Networks - GANs**) to substitute a person's likeness.
*   **GPAI (Global Partnership on AI):** A multi-stakeholder initiative (with India as a founding member) to bridge the gap between AI theory and practice.
*   **INDIAai:** India’s national AI portal, a one-stop digital platform for AI-related developments.

### 2. Blockchain Technology & E-Governance
*   **Unified Blockchain Framework:** Developed by **C-DAC** to offer **Blockchain-as-a-Service (BaaS)**, allowing government departments to host smart contracts and secure applications without building infrastructure from scratch.
*   **Web 3.0:** A decentralized version of the internet where users have ownership of their data, powered by peer-to-peer transactions and blockchain (unlike the centralized Web 2.0).

### 3. National Quantum Mission (NQM)
*   **Objective:** To scale up R&D in Quantum Technology (2023-2031).
*   **Targets:** Developing intermediate-scale quantum computers (50-1000 physical qubits) and secure satellite-based quantum communications over 2000 km.
*   **Qubits:** Unlike classical bits (0 or 1), qubits use **Superposition** and **Entanglement** to represent multiple states simultaneously.

## II. Energy: Shale Gas & Nuclear Energy
Energy security in India involves both non-conventional sources like Shale Gas and advanced indigenous nuclear power.

### 1. Nuclear Energy
*   **KAPP-3 (Kakrapar Nuclear Power Plant Unit-3):** India's first largest indigenous **700 MWe Pressurised Heavy Water Reactor (PHWR)** in Gujarat. It uses natural uranium as fuel and heavy water as both moderator and coolant.
*   **Small Modular Reactors (SMRs):** Advanced reactors with power capacity up to 300 MWe. They are modular (factory-assembled) and offer higher safety features and flexible deployment.
*   **Net Energy Gain (NEG):** A major milestone in nuclear fusion research where the energy produced exceeds the energy consumed to trigger the reaction. India participates in this via the **ITER project** (France).
*   **Nuclear Medicine:** Use of radioactive tracers (e.g., **Cobalt-60** for radiation therapy) to assess bodily functions and treat diseases.

### 2. Shale Gas & Coalbed Methane (CBM)
*   **Shale Gas:** Natural gas trapped within fine-grained sedimentary rocks called shales. It is extracted using **Hydraulic Fracturing (Fracking)**.
*   **Guar Gum:** Extracted from cluster beans, it is used as a thickening agent in fracking fluids to suspend sand (proppant) used to keep fissures open.
*   **Coalbed Methane (CBM):** Methane gas extracted from coal seams.

## III. Space Technology & Remote Sensing
India's space program has shifted from exploration to capability building in navigation and deep-space missions.

### 1. High-Profile Missions
*   **Chandrayaan-3:** India became the first country to soft-land on the **Lunar South Pole** (August 2023). The mission consisted of a lander (Vikram) and a rover (Pragyan).
*   **Aditya-L1:** India's first solar observatory mission placed at the **Lagrangian Point L1** (1.5 million km from Earth) to study the Sun's corona and solar winds.
*   **Gaganyaan:** India's first human spaceflight mission aiming to send a 3-member crew to a Low Earth Orbit (LEO) of 400 km for 3 days.

### 2. Navigation & Remote Sensing
*   **NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation):** India's independent satellite navigation system (IRNSS) covering India and a region up to 1500 km around it.
*   **James Webb Space Telescope (JWST):** A successor to Hubble, it uses infrared sensors to look back in time at the earliest galaxies.

## IV. Biotechnology & Genetics
Biotechnology applications span from agriculture (GMOs) to life-saving medical therapies.

### 1. Genetic Engineering & GMOs
*   **Genome Editing:** Methods like **CRISPR/Cas9** allow precise alterations to an organism's DNA. Unlike traditional GMOs, it doesn't always involve foreign DNA (transgenes).
*   **Bt Cotton:** The only GM crop commercially approved in India. It contains **Cry genes** from the bacterium *Bacillus thuringiensis* to resist bollworms.
*   **GM Mustard (DMH-11):** An indigenous transgenic mustard variety developed by Delhi University using three genes (*barnase, barstar, and bar*) for hybridization.

### 2. Medical Biotechnology
*   **Insulin Production:** Produced using recombinant DNA technology by inserting human insulin DNA into **E. coli** plasmids (first achieved by Eli Lilly in 1983).
*   **Gene Therapy:** Correcting genetic defects by delivering functional genes. Example: Treatment for **ADA (Adenosine Deaminase) deficiency**.
*   **Three Parent Baby:** A technique called **Mitochondrial Donation Treatment (MDT)** to prevent the transmission of mitochondrial diseases from mother to child.

## V. Defence Technology
India focuses on indigenous development through programs like DRDO's "Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme" (IGMDP).

### 1. Missile Systems
*   **S-400 Triumf:** A long-range surface-to-air missile (SAM) system procured from Russia.
*   **Pralay:** India's first tactical quasi-ballistic missile for battlefield use (range 150-500 km).
*   **Hypersonic Missiles:** Weapons that travel at speeds exceeding **Mach 5** while maintaining maneuverability (unlike typical ballistic missiles).

### 2. Naval & Aerial Platforms
*   **Project 75I:** A project to build advanced diesel-electric submarines with **AIP (Air-Independent Propulsion)** for longer underwater endurance.
*   **INS Imphal:** An indigenous stealth guided-missile destroyer (Visakhapatnam-class).
*   **TAPAS BH-201:** A Medium Altitude Long Endurance (MALE) Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) developed by DRDO.
