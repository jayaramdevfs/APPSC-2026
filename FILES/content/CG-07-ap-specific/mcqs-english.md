# Q1
**Topic:** AP History & Culture
**Sub-topic:** Dynasties — Satavahanas
**Source:** AP SCERT Class 6 Social Studies
**Difficulty:** L1
**Language:** English
**Question:** Who was the greatest ruler of the Satavahana dynasty, known as the 'destroyer of the pride of Kshatriyas'?
A. Simuka
B. Gautamiputra Satakarni
C. Vashishtiputra Pulumayi
D. Hala
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Gautamiputra Satakarni (106–130 CE) was the greatest ruler of the Satavahana dynasty. He defeated the Shakas, Yavanas, and Pahlavas, and is described as 'destroyer of the pride of Kshatriyas' in the Nasik inscription of his mother Gautami Balashri.

1. The famous Kohinoor diamond was discovered at the Kolluru diamond mines during this period.
2. The Qutb Shahi rulers maintained a standing army throughout their rule.
3. Sultan Quli Qutb Shah declared independence from the Bahmanis in 1518 C.E.

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
