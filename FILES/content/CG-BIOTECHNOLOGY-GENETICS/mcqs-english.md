Q1. The restriction endonuclease EcoRI recognises the specific palindromic nucleotide sequence in DNA. Which of the following is the correct sequence?
(a) 5' - GATATC - 3'
(b) 5' - GAATTC - 3'
(c) 5' - GTATAC - 3'
(d) 5' - GGATCC - 3'

Answer: (b)
Explanation: EcoRI specifically recognises the palindromic sequence 5'-GAATTC-3' and cuts between G and A. The other sequences are recognised by different restriction enzymes.

Q2. Consider the following statements regarding the Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR):
1. The denaturation step is carried out at a high temperature (around 94°C) to separate the two strands of DNA.
2. DNA polymerase isolated from E. coli is used for the extension step because of its high efficiency.
3. The process of DNA replication in PCR is repeated many times to amplify the DNA segment.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (c)
Explanation: Statement 1 is correct. Statement 2 is incorrect because the DNA polymerase used in PCR is Taq polymerase, isolated from the thermophilic bacterium Thermus aquaticus, as it remains active during high temperatures. Statement 3 is correct as the goal of PCR is amplification.

Q3. In the context of genetically modified crops, what is the primary mechanism of action of the Bt toxin?
(a) It inhibits the photosynthesis process in the insect pests.
(b) It creates pores in the midgut epithelial cells of the insect, leading to cell lysis.
(c) It blocks the respiratory chain in the mitochondria of pests.
(d) It acts as a neurotoxin that paralyzes the insect's nervous system.

Answer: (b)
Explanation: The Bt toxin is ingested in its inactive form. The alkaline pH of the insect's gut solubilises the crystals into an active toxin. This active toxin binds to the surface of midgut epithelial cells and creates pores that cause cell swelling and lysis, eventually causing the death of the insect.

Q5. Which of the following is NOT a correct statement regarding the application of RNA interference (RNAi)?
(a) It is a method of cellular defense present in all eukaryotic organisms.
(b) It involves the silencing of a specific mRNA due to a complementary dsRNA molecule.
(c) It has been primarily used to develop tobacco plants resistant to the nematode Meloidegyne incognita.
(d) The technique requires the use of Agrobacterium vectors to introduce restriction enzymes into the host plant.

Answer: (d)
Explanation: RNAi technique uses Agrobacterium vectors to introduce nematode-specific genes into the host plant to produce both sense and antisense RNA, which form the dsRNA that triggers RNAi. It does not introduce restriction enzymes for this purpose. The other statements are correct.

Q6. Compared to the maturation of human insulin in the body, the genetically engineered insulin produced by Eli Lilly using E. coli:
(a) Contains the extra C-peptide sequence that makes it more stable.
(b) Is synthesised directly without the C-peptide stretch by creating separate DNA sequences for A and B chains.
(c) Requires post-translational modification inside the bacterial host to join the A, B, and C chains.
(d) Is identical to pro-insulin and needs to be activated in the human bloodstream.

Answer: (b)
Explanation: In humans, insulin is synthesised as a pro-hormone containing an extra C-peptide, which is removed during maturation. Eli Lilly prepared two DNA sequences corresponding to A and B, chains of human insulin and introduced them in plasmids of E. coli to produce insulin chains separately, which were then extracted and combined by creating disulfide bonds.

Q9. In the context of the controversy surrounding naturally occurring biological resources, what does the term 'Biopiracy' refer to?
(a) The unauthorized production of generic medicines by developing nations without paying royalties.
(b) The theft of laboratory-created recombinant DNA plasmids by rival pharmaceutical companies.
(c) The use of bio-resources by multinational companies without proper authorization from the countries and people concerned.
(d) The deliberate release of genetically modified organisms into the wild to alter ecosystems.

Answer: (c)
Explanation: Biopiracy is the term used to refer to the use of bio-resources by multinational companies and other organisations without proper authorisation from the countries and people concerned without compensatory payment. Examples include US patents filed for traditional Indian resources like Basmati rice, Neem, and Turmeric.
