# Q1
**Topic:** Geography

**Source:** NCERT Class 11 - India Physical Environment

**Question:** Which of the following is the northernmost range of the Himalayas, known for its high peaks and perennial snow?
A. Himachal
B. Himadri
C. Shiwalik
D. Purvanchal
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The northernmost range of the Himalayas is the Great or Inner Himalayas, also known as 'Himadri'. It is the most continuous range consisting of the loftiest peaks with an average height of 6,000 metres.

# Q3
**Topic:** Geography
**Sub-topic:** Physiographic Divisions of India
**Source:** NCERT Class 11 - India Physical Environment
**Difficulty:** L3
**Language:** English
**Question:** Consider the following statements regarding the Western and Eastern Ghats:
1. Western Ghats are continuous and can be crossed through passes only.
2. The Eastern Ghats are higher in average elevation than the Western Ghats.
3. Anai Mudi is the highest peak of the Western Ghats.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
A. 1 and 2 only
B. 2 and 3 only
C. 1 and 3 only
D. 1, 2 and 3
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Western Ghats (average elevation 900-1600m) are indeed higher than Eastern Ghats (average elevation 600m). Western Ghats are continuous while Eastern Ghats are discontinuous and irregular, dissected by rivers. Anai Mudi (2,695m) is the highest peak in the Western Ghats.

# Q9

**Difficulty:** L2
**Language:** English
**Question:** The 'Inter Tropical Convergence Zone' (ITCZ) is a low-pressure zone located at the:
A. Tropic of Cancer
B. Equator
C. Arctic Circle
D. Tropic of Capricorn
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The ITCZ is a broad trough of low pressure in equatorial latitudes. This is where the northeast and the southeast trade winds converge. Its seasonal shift plays a vital role in the Indian Monsoon.

# Q10
**Topic:** Geography
**Sub-topic:** Climate of India
**Source:** NCERT Class 11 - India Physical Environment
**Difficulty:** L3
**Language:** English
**Question:** In the context of the Indian Monsoon, what does the term 'El Niño' signify?
A. A cooling of the sea surface temperatures in the Arabian Sea.
B. A periodic development of a warm ocean current along the coast of Peru.
C. A high-pressure system over the Tibetan Plateau.
D. A delay in the shift of the Sub-Tropical Westerly Jet stream.
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** El Niño is the name given to the periodic development of a warm ocean current along the coast of Peru as a temporary replacement of the cold Peruvian current. It is associated with changes in pressure conditions and generally results in a weaker monsoon in India.

A. Polar Jet Stream
B. Easterly Jet Stream
C. Sub-Tropical Westerly Jet Stream
D. Somali Jet Stream
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The onset of the monsoon is associated with the withdrawal of the Sub-Tropical Westerly Jet Stream from the northern plains and its shift to the north of the Himalayas. Simultaneously, the Tropical Easterly Jet stream sets in over the peninsula.

**Question:** Why does the river Kaveri carry water throughout the year with less fluctuation compared to other Peninsular rivers?
A. It is fed by melting Himalayan glaciers.
B. It receives rainfall from both the South-West and North-East monsoons.
C. It flows through a very deep rift valley that prevents evaporation.
D. It has a very large number of perennial tributaries.
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The upper catchment area of the Kaveri receives rainfall during the South-West monsoon (summer), while its lower part receives rainfall during the North-East monsoon (winter). Thus, it remains relatively perennial.
