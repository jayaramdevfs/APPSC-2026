Q1. According to the AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25, what percentage of Andhra Pradesh's population is dependent on agriculture?
(a) 40%
(b) 50%
(c) 60%
(d) 70%

Answer: (c)
Explanation: Agriculture is a key sector in Andhra Pradesh, supporting 60% of the population. It remains the largest contributor to the rural economy. However, agriculture's share of GVA (35.29%) is much lower than the population it supports, highlighting a productivity challenge.

Q2. Consider the following statements about food grain production in Andhra Pradesh during 2024-25 (2nd Advance Estimates):
1. Total food grain production is estimated at 161.86 lakh tonnes, a 12.94% increase over 2023-24.
2. Rabi paddy showed higher area growth (20.73%) compared to Kharif paddy (4.23%).
3. The overall area under food grains decreased by 12.85% compared to the previous year.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (b)
Explanation: Statement 1 is correct — food grain production rose to 161.86 lakh tonnes (+12.94%). Statement 2 is correct — Rabi paddy area growth (20.73%) far exceeded Kharif (4.23%). Statement 3 is incorrect — the area under food grains INCREASED by 12.85% (from 33.24 to 37.51 lakh hectares), not decreased.

Q4. Which of the following is NOT one of the six Agro-Climatic Zones of Andhra Pradesh?
(a) North Coastal Zone
(b) Scarce Rainfall Zone
(c) Deccan Plateau Zone
(d) High Altitude and Tribal Areas Zone

Answer: (c)
Explanation: AP has six agro-climatic zones: (1) North Coastal, (2) Godavari, (3) Krishna, (4) Southern, (5) Scarce Rainfall, and (6) High Altitude & Tribal Areas. "Deccan Plateau Zone" is not one of AP's defined agro-climatic zones — it is a general geographical concept. The Scarce Rainfall Zone (Kurnool, Anantapuramu) covers parts of the Deccan Plateau but is specifically named differently.

Q7. During June–December 2024, Andhra Pradesh received an average total rainfall of 960.3 mm. This was:
(a) 16.5% below normal, as both monsoons were deficient
(b) 11.7% above normal, with the South-West Monsoon being excess and North-East Monsoon slightly deficient
(c) 18.6% above normal, as both monsoons brought excess rainfall
(d) Exactly at normal levels, with the South-West excess compensating for North-East deficit

Answer: (b)
Explanation: Total rainfall (June–Dec 2024) was 960.3 mm vs normal 860.0 mm — 11.7% above normal. The SW monsoon brought +18.6% excess (681.6 mm vs normal 574.7 mm), while the NE monsoon was slightly deficient at -2.3% (278.7 mm vs normal 285.3 mm). Option (c) is incorrect because the NE monsoon was deficient.

Q8. Andhra Pradesh's agricultural strategy includes promoting Zero-Budget Natural Farming (ZBNF). In the context of AP's agriculture policy, which of the following most accurately explains the rationale for promoting ZBNF?
(a) To completely replace irrigation-based farming in the state within 5 years
(b) To minimize adverse climate impacts and reduce dependence on chemical farming for long-term farmer welfare
(c) To convert all existing paddy fields to organic certification for export purposes
(d) To reduce the agricultural contribution to GSDP by shifting labor to the services sector

Answer: (b)
Explanation: The AP government promotes ZBNF specifically "to ensure the long-term welfare and prosperity of farmers while minimizing adverse climate impacts." It is not about replacing irrigation-based farming (a) or converting to organic for exports (c). It is a sustainability initiative, not a GSDP reduction strategy (d). Under APCNF, 25,000 acres are being brought under natural farming.

Q10. In the context of AP's agricultural transformation, which combination of long-term trends most accurately reflects the state's agricultural challenge?
(a) Increasing landholdings + Increasing production + Decreasing net area sown
(b) Decreasing average landholding size + Decreasing net area sown + Yet increasing food grain production
(c) Increasing net area sown + Decreasing landholdings + Decreasing production
(d) Stable net area sown + Stable landholdings + Rapidly increasing production

Answer: (b)
Explanation: AP faces a complex situation: average landholding size has decreased (from 0.94 to 0.86 ha), net area sown has declined significantly (from 67.26 to 49.55 lakh hectares), yet food grain production has increased (161.86 lakh tonnes in 2024-25). This paradox — less land but more production — indicates improved productivity but also highlights the pressure of fragmentation and urbanization on agricultural land.
