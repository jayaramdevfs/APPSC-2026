## 6. Horticulture

- AP is the **largest fruit producer** in India (Source: AP Survey Ch.1).
- Horticulture area: **18.23 lakh hectares**, production: **365.92 lakh MT**, contributing **15.6%** of national output.
- AP excels in: oil palm, papaya, lime, cocoa, tomato, chillies productivity.
- AP ranks **2nd** in: mango, sweet orange, turmeric.
- In 2024-25: 1,43,329 acres converted to remunerative horticulture crops.
- Micro-irrigation: 2.05 lakh acres covered, benefiting 75,035 farmers with ₹793.67 crore subsidy.
- Oil palm area expanded by **39,502 acres**.

## 9. Thematic Understanding (for Mains)

### Agriculture-Led Growth Paradox
- Agriculture supports 60% of population but contributes only 35.29% of GVA — highlighting the productivity challenge.
- Average landholding decreased from 0.94 ha (2015-16) to 0.86 ha (2021-22) — fragmentation issue.
- Net area sown has been **declining** (from 67.26 lakh hectares in 2008-09 to 49.55 in 2023-24), yet production is rising — indicating productivity improvement but also urbanization pressure.

### Climate & Monsoon Dependence
- Despite irrigation infrastructure development, AP's agriculture remains highly monsoon-dependent.
- SW monsoon contributes ~71% of annual rainfall; excess/deficit directly impacts Kharif production.
- The state aims to de-risk agriculture through crop diversification, micro-irrigation, and ZBNF.

### Tenant Farmer Protection
- CCRCs are a unique AP innovation — providing formal recognition to tenant farmers who don't own land but cultivate it.
- This enables tenant farmers to access institutional credit — a significant policy reform.
