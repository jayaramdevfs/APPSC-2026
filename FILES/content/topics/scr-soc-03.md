## 1. Public Policies and Welfare Programmes

### National Level Schemes
- **MGNREGA (2005):** Ensures 100 days of guaranteed wage employment to rural households.
- **PM-KISAN:** Income support of ₹6,000/year to all landholding farmer families.
- **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY):** Largest health insurance scheme providing ₹5 Lakh cover per family/year.
- **PDS (Public Distribution System):** Food security through subsidized grains.

### Andhra Pradesh Specifc (Navaratnalu)
- **Amma Vodi:** Financial assistance to mothers for educating children.
- **YSR Rythu Bharosa:** Investment support for farmers.
- **Jagananna Vidya Deevena:** Full fee reimbursement for students.

## 2. Provisions for Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST)

### Constitutional Provisions
- **Article 17:** Abolition of Untouchability.
- **Article 46:** Promotion of educational and economic interests of SC, ST and other weaker sections.
- **Article 330:** Reservation of seats for SC/ST in the Lok Sabha.
- **Article 332:** Reservation of seats for SC/ST in State Legislative Assemblies.
- **Article 335:** Claims of SC/ST to services and posts.
- **Article 338 & 338A:** National Commission for SC and National Commission for ST.

### Statutory Provisions
- **SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989:** Provides specialized courts and stringent punishments for offences against SC/ST members.

---

## 3. Provisions for Minorities and Backward Classes (BC)

### Minorities
- **Article 29:** Protection of interests of minorities (Language, Script, Culture).
- **Article 30:** Right of minorities to establish and administer educational institutions.
- **National Commission for Minorities Act, 1992.**

### Backward Classes (BC)
- **Article 16(4):** Reservation in public employment for any backward class not adequately represented.
- **Article 340:** Appointment of a commission to investigate the conditions of backward classes.
- **Article 338B:** National Commission for Backward Classes (added by 102nd Amendment Act).

---

## 4. Provisions for Women, Disabled and Children

### Women
- **Article 15(3):** Special provisions for women and children.
- **Article 39(a) & (d):** Livelihood and Equal pay for equal work.
- **Article 42:** Provision for just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief.

### Disabled (Divyangjan)
- **Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016:** Increased reservation in government jobs from 3% to 4% and identified 21 types of disabilities.

### Children
- **Article 21A:** Right to Education (6-14 years).
- **Article 24:** Prohibition of employment in hazardous industries.
- **Article 45:** Early childhood care and education.
