## 1. Natural Resources & Management
Based on NCERT Class 8 (Ch 1-2) and NCERT 10 (Ch 1)

### Classification of Resources
- **On Origin**: Biotic (living) and Abiotic (non-living).
- **On Exhaustibility**: Renewable (Solar, Wind) and Non-Renewable (Coal, Petroleum).
- **On Ownership**: Individual, Community, National, and International (beyond 200 nautical miles EEZ).
- **On Development**: Potential (unutilized), Developed, Stock, and Reserves.

### Land and Soil Resources
- **Land Use in India**: Net Sown Area (~46%), Forests (~23%), Barren/Waste Land.
- **Soil Types**:
    - **Alluvial**: Most fertile; Northern Plains & River Deltas.
    - **Black (Regur)**: Ideal for cotton; Deccan Trap (Basalt).
    - **Red and Yellow**: Iron-rich; Peninsular plateau.
    - **Laterite**: Formed by leaching in high rain areas (Cashew, Tea/Coffee).
- **AP Angle**: Land utilization in AP: **30.4% Cultivated** (49.55 lakh ha); Forests 22.63%.

## 2. Agriculture: India & Andhra Pradesh
Based on NCERT 10 (Ch 4) and AP Survey 2024-25

### Farming Types
- **Primitive Subsistence**: Shifting cultivation (Jhumming in NE, **Podu in AP/Telangana**).
- **Intensive Subsistence**: High pressure on land; high manual labor.
- **Commercial**: Plantation (Tea, Coffee, Rubber).

### Major Crops (India)
- **Kharif** (June-Oct): Rice, Maize, Jowar, Bajra, Tur, Cotton, Jute.
- **Rabi** (Oct-March): Wheat, Barley, Gram, Mustard.
- **Zaid** (Summer): Watermelon, Cucumber.

### Andhra Pradesh Agriculture Stats (2024-25)
- **Contribution**: Supports 60% of population.
- **Food Grain Production**: 161.86 lakh tonnes (12.9% increase from last year).
- **Paddy**: Main crop; 127.15 lakh tonnes.
- **Horticulture**: AP is **India's largest fruit producer** (15.6% of national output). 1st in Oil Palm, Papaya, Lime, Chillies. 2nd in Mango, Turmeric.
- **Livestock**: **1st in Egg production** (2548.74 lakh eggs), 5th in Meat, 7th in Milk.
- **Fisheries**: AP contributes **30% of national fish production** and 31% of shrimp exports.

## 3. Minerals and Energy Resources
Based on NCERT 10 (Ch 5)

### Metallic Minerals
- **Iron Ore**: India is rich in Hematite/Magnetite. Major belts: Odisha-Jharkhand, Durg-Bastar, Bellary-Chikkamagaluru.
- **Manganese**: Odisha is largest producer.
- **Bauxite (Aluminum)**: Amarkantak plateau, Odisha (Panchpatmali).

### Non-Metallic & Energy
- **Mica**: Rajasthan, Jharkhand, **Nellore (AP)**.
- **Coal**: Gondwana (Damodar valley) and Tertiary coal (NE). Jharkhand is leading.
- **Petroleum**: Mumbai High, Gujarat, Assam. **Krishna-Godavari Basin** (Offshore).

### AP Mineral Wealth
- **Red Sanders**: Unique to Seshachalam Hills.
- **Mica**: Nellore belt is world-famous.
- **Limestone**: Large reserves in Palnadu, Kadapa, Kurnool (supports cement industry).
- **Barytes**: Mangampet (Kadapa) - World's largest deposit.

## 4. Manufacturing Industries
Based on NCERT 10 (Ch 6) and AP Survey 2024-25

### Key Clusters
1. **Iron & Steel**: TATA (Jamshedpur), Bhilai, Durgapur, **Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (RINL)**.
2. **Cotton Textiles**: Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Coimbatore.
3. **IT & Electronics**: Bengaluru (Silicon Valley of India).

### Industrial Development in AP (2024-25)
- **GSDP Contribution**: Manufacturing contributes ~11%.
- **Ease of Doing Business (EoDB)**: AP Ranks **No.1 in India**.
- **Industrial Corridors**:
    - **VCIC**: Visakhapatnam-Chennai Industrial Corridor.
    - **CBIC**: Chennai-Bangalore Industrial Corridor (Krishnapatnam node).
    - **HBIC**: Hyderabad-Bangalore Industrial Corridor (Orvakal node).
- **Special Economic Zones (SEZs)**: Strategic focus on Pharmaceuticals (Vizag), Textiles, and Electronics.

## 5. Lifelines of National Economy (Trade & Transport)
Based on NCERT 10 (Ch 7) and AP Survey 2024-25

- **Roads**: Golden Quadrilateral (Delhi-Mumbai-Chennai-Kolkata).
- **Ports**: 12 Major Ports. 
    - **Visakhapatnam**: Deepest landlocked and protected port.
    - **Krishnapatnam & Kakinada**: Major private/non-major ports in AP.
- **Coastline**: AP has the **third-longest coastline** in India (974 km).
- **Irrigation**: **Polavaram Project** status (Approx 78% work completed).
