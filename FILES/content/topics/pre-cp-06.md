# India's Foreign Policy & International Relations

## 1. Foundations of India's Foreign Policy

India's foreign policy rests on five foundational pillars established at independence:

- **Non-Alignment**: Staying independent of Cold War power blocs. India was a founding member of the **Non-Aligned Movement (NAM)** — 1961 Belgrade Conference.
- **Panchsheel (Five Principles of Peaceful Coexistence)**: Signed with China in **1954** (Tibet Trade Agreement).
  1. Mutual respect for territorial integrity and sovereignty.
  2. Mutual non-aggression.
  3. Mutual non-interference in internal affairs.
  4. Equality and mutual benefit.
  5. Peaceful coexistence.
- **Anti-colonialism & Anti-imperialism**: Support for freedom movements worldwide.
- **Respect for International Law (UN Charter)**.
- **Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam** — "The world is one family" (G20 theme, India's Presidency 2023).

## 2. India's Key Foreign Policy Doctrines & Initiatives

| Doctrine/Initiative | Year | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gujral Doctrine** | 1997 | Non-reciprocal concessions to smaller neighbours (Pakistan excluded). Unilateral goodwill. |
| **Look East Policy** | 1991 | Engagement with ASEAN. Upgraded to **Act East Policy** in 2014. |
| **Neighbourhood First** | 2014 | Modi govt. — prioritise SAARC neighbours in diplomacy. |
| **SAGAR** | 2015 | Security and Growth for All in the Region — India's Indian Ocean strategy. |
| **Indo-Pacific Vision** | 2018+ | Free, Open, Inclusive Indo-Pacific — countering China's influence. |

## 3. India and the United Nations

- **Founding Member**: India was a founding member of the UN (1945), signed the UN Charter.
- **Security Council**: India is NOT a permanent member (P5 = USA, UK, France, Russia, China). India has been a non-permanent member 8 times.
- **India's Stand on UNSC Reform**: India leads the G4 group (with Japan, Germany, Brazil) seeking permanent UNSC seats.
- **UN Peacekeeping**: India is one of the **largest contributors** of troops to UN peacekeeping missions.

---

## 4. Nuclear Policy

- **NFU (No First Use)**: India's declared nuclear doctrine — will not use nuclear weapons first, but will retaliate massively if attacked.
- **CTBT**: India has NOT signed the Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty.
- **NPT**: India has NOT signed the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (argues it's discriminatory).
- **Indo-US Nuclear Deal (2008)**: 123 Agreement — allowed India to access civilian nuclear technology while keeping its weapons program outside IAEA scrutiny.

> 💡 **EXAM TRAP**: India's 1998 tests (Pokhran-II / Operation Shakti) led to US & other sanctions, which were only lifted post the 2008 nuclear deal.

## 6. India-AP Specific International Linkages

- **Vizag** (Visakhapatnam) is a key port for India's trade with Southeast Asia under Act East Policy.
- **Krishnapatnam Port** (AP) serves as a strategic hub for trade with East and Southeast Asia.
- **AP's Blue Economy Initiative**: Aligned with India's SAGAR doctrine for Indian Ocean development.
