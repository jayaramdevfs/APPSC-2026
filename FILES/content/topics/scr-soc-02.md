## 1. Casteism, Communalism and Regionalisation

### Casteism
- **Definition:** The blind group loyalty to one's own caste, often leading to prejudice and discrimination against other castes.
- **Impact on Politics:** "Casteism in politics and politicization of caste." Political parties often mobilize voters based on caste identities (Bank Voting).
- **Scheduled Castes (Dalits):** Historically marginalized groups. The **Mandal Commission (1979)** recommended 27% reservation for OBCs, which was implemented in 1990, leading to nationwide protests and change in social dynamics.

### Communalism
- **Definition:** An ideology that assumes that people who follow the same religion have common political, economic, and social interests.
- **Consequences:** Religious riots (e.g., 1984 Anti-Sikh riots, 2002 Gujarat riots). 
- **The Concept of Secularism:** India follows "Sarva Dharma Sambhava" (Equality of all religions).

### Regionalisation
- **Definition:** A feeling of affinity and devotion to a particular region or state, often prioritized over national interest.
- **Causes:** Economic disparity, linguism (demand for states based on language), and cultural identity.
- **Example:** The formation of separate states like Telangana (2014) based on regional aspirations.

## 2. Crime against Women
- **Types of Crime:**
  - **Domestic Violence:** Physical, sexual, or mental abuse within the household. (PWDVA Act, 2005).
  - **Sexual Harassment:** Includes harassment at the workplace (Vishakha Guidelines / POSH Act 2013).
  - **Dowry Deaths:** Deaths related to harassment for dowry (Section 304B of IPC).
  - **Acid Attacks:** Crimes targeting women with corrosive substances (Section 326A of IPC).
- **Protective Measures:** National Commission for Women (NCW), Women Helplines (181/1091).

## 3. Child Abuse and Child Labour

### Child Labour
- **Definition:** Work that deprives children of their childhood, potential, and dignity.
- **Constitutional Provision:** **Article 24** prohibits the employment of children below 14 years in factories/mines.
- **Legislation:** **Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986** (Amended in 2016 to prohibit employment below 14 in ALL occupations).

### Child Abuse
- **POCOSO Act (2012):** The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, providing a comprehensive legal framework to protect children from sexual assault and harassment.

## 4. Youth Unrest and Agitation
- **Causes:** Unemployment, lack of vocational training, educational system flaws, and political exploitation.
- **Nature of Agitation:** Protests for reservations (e.g., Maratha, Jat, Kapu agitations), student union conflicts, and anti-corruption movements (e.g., Jan Lokpal movement).
- **Solution:** Skill India Mission, National Youth Policy (2014) to enhance youth empowerment and employability.
