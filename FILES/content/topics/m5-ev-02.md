# Conservation & Natural Resources

## 1. Forest Resources

### Forest Cover in India (FSI Report 2023):
- **Total Forest & Tree Cover**: ~80 million hectares (~24% of India's geographic area).
- **Very Dense Forest (VDF)**: Canopy density > 70%.
- **Moderately Dense Forest (MDF)**: 40–70% canopy.
- **Open Forest**: 10–40% canopy density.

> 💡 **EXAM TRAP**: The legal definition of "forest" in India is based on the 1996 Supreme Court judgement (Godavarman case) — any land recorded as "forest" in any government record, regardless of canopy cover.

### Forest Types in India (Champion & Seth classification):
1. Tropical Wet Evergreen — Western Ghats, Andaman & Nicobar
2. Tropical Semi-Evergreen — Northeast
3. Tropical Moist Deciduous — Most of India, including AP (Sal, Teak)
4. Tropical Dry Deciduous — Central India, AP interior (Teak, Tendu)
5. Tropical Thorn Forest — Rajasthan, Gujarat, AP's Rayalaseema
6. Montane Subtropical — Himalayan foothills
7. Alpine — High Himalayas (above treeline)

### AP Forest Cover:
- AP has significant Eastern Ghats forest cover.
- **Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve**: 3,568 sq km — largest tiger reserve in India.
- **Papikondalu Wildlife Sanctuary**: Along Godavari river.

## 2. Water Resources

### Types of Water Resources:
- **Surface Water**: Rivers, lakes, reservoirs, ponds.
- **Groundwater**: Stored in aquifers. India is the **world's largest extractor of groundwater**.
- **Rainwater**: Primary source; India's average rainfall = ~1,170 mm/year.

### India's Major River Systems:
| System | Key Rivers | Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Himalayan** | Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra, Indus | Perennial (glacier-fed + rain-fed) |
| **Peninsular** | Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery, Mahanadi | Seasonal (rain-fed only) |

### AP's River Systems:
- **Godavari**: Largest river of AP; called "Dakshin Ganga." Polavaram dam under construction.
- **Krishna**: 2nd major river; Nagarjuna Sagar and Srisailam are on this river.
- **Penna (Pennar)**: Flows through Kadapa, Nellore; largely seasonal.
- **Vamsadhara & Nagavali**: Northern rivers (Srikakulam, Vizianagaram).

### Water Conservation Strategies:
- **Watershed Management**: Treating entire catchment areas.
- **Rainwater Harvesting**: Recharge pits, check dams, percolation tanks.
- **Interlinking of Rivers (ILR)**: Ambitious project to link surplus river basins with deficit ones. Ken-Betwa is first link to be executed.
- **Jal Jeevan Mission (2019)**: "Har Ghar Jal" — piped water to every rural household by 2024.
- **Jal Shakti Abhiyan**: Catch the Rain — water conservation campaign.

## 3. Mineral Resources

### Classification:
- **Metallic**: Iron ore, Bauxite, Copper, Gold, Manganese, Zinc.
- **Non-Metallic**: Limestone, Mica, Gypsum, Graphite.
- **Fuel Minerals**: Coal, Petroleum, Natural Gas (covered separately under energy).
- **Atomic Minerals**: Uranium (Jaduguda, Jharkhand; Tummalapalle, AP), Thorium (Kerala coast).

### AP's Mineral Wealth:
- **Baryte (Barytes)**: AP is the world's largest producer. Cuddapah basin.
- **Limestone**: Abundant; basis of cement industry (Guntur, Kurnool, Kadapa).
- **Mica**: Nellore district is famous for sheet mica.
- **Uranium**: Tummalapalle in Kadapa district — one of India's largest uranium deposits.
- **Manganese**: Srikakulam district.
- **Graphite**: Srikakulam, Vizianagaram.
- **Granite (Dimension Stone)**: India is world's 2nd largest granite exporter; AP (Kurnool, Kadapa, Chittoor) has major reserves.
- **Black Diamond (Coal)**: AP has limited coal; mostly Singareni collieries in Telangana.

## 4. Energy Resources

### Conventional Sources:
- **Coal**: India has 4th largest coal reserves. Major states: Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh.
- **Petroleum**: Major reserves: Bombay High (offshore), Rajasthan (Barmer), Gujarat.
- **Natural Gas**: Krishna-Godavari (KG) Basin — AP. Reliance's D6 block.

### Renewable Energy:
| Source | India Installed Capacity (approx. 2024) | Target 2030 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Solar | ~80 GW | 280 GW |
| Wind | ~47 GW | 140 GW |
| Hydropower | ~47 GW | 60 GW |
| Biopower & Others | ~11 GW | 20 GW |

- **Green Hydrogen Mission (2023)**: India aims to produce **5 MMT** of Green Hydrogen/year by 2030. AP is a key state.

## 5. Conservation Initiatives

### Project Tiger (1973):
- India has **55 Tiger Reserves** (as of 2024).
- India holds ~70% of the world's wild tiger population.
- **Tiger Census (2022)**: India has **3,167 tigers** — highest ever.

### Project Elephant (1992):
- 33 Elephant Reserves in India.
- India has ~60% of Asia's elephant population.

### Project Crocodile (1975):
- Protection of Mugger, Saltwater, and Gharial crocodiles.
- **Gharial**: Critically Endangered; found in Chambal river.

### Wetland Conservation:
- **Ramsar Convention (1971)**: India has **75 Ramsar sites** (largest in the world as of 2023).
- **Montreux Record**: List of Ramsar sites facing ecological change. **Loktak Lake** (Manipur) and **Keoladeo Ghana** (Rajasthan) are on this record.

### Community Reserves & Conservation Reserves:
- Added by the Wildlife Protection Act Amendment (2002).
- **Community Reserves**: On community/private land with community involvement.
- **Conservation Reserves**: On government land adjacent to PAs.
