# Science & Technology — Key Concepts for APPSC

## 1. Space Technology

### ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation):
- **Headquarters**: Bengaluru.
- **Founded**: 1969 by Dr. Vikram Sarabhai (Father of Indian Space Programme).
- **Current Chairman**: V. Narayanan (2024).

### Major Missions:

| Mission | Year | Achievement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aryabhata** | 1975 | India's first satellite |
| **SLV-3** | 1980 | First indigenous launch vehicle (by APJ Abdul Kalam) |
| **Chandrayaan-1** | 2008 | Discovered water molecules on the Moon |
| **Mangalyaan (MOM)** | 2013 | India's first interplanetary mission; Mars orbit in first attempt |
| **Chandrayaan-3** | 2023 | Soft landing on Moon's south pole — **India 1st country to do so** |
| **Aditya-L1** | 2023 | India's first solar observatory at L1 Lagrange point |
| **GSLV Mk-III / LVM-3** | — | Heavy-lift launch vehicle; also used for commercial launches |

> 💡 **EXAM TRAP**: Chandrayaan-1 found water molecules in 2009 (data analysis) but the mission was launched in **2008**. Chandrayaan-3 landed near the south pole on **23 August 2023**.

## 2. Defence Technology

### Key Developments:
- **DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation)**: India's primary defence R&D agency.
- **Agni Missiles**: Ballistic missiles. Agni-V is India's ICBM (range 5,000+ km).
- **BrahMos**: Jointly developed with Russia. World's fastest supersonic cruise missile (~2.8 Mach).
- **HAL (Hindustan Aeronautics Limited)**: Indigenous aircraft production — Tejas (Light Combat Aircraft).
- **Tejas**: India's first indigenously designed and developed combat aircraft.
- **INS Vikrant**: India's first indigenous aircraft carrier; commissioned in **2022**.
- **Nuclear Triad**: India has achieved a nuclear triad (land + air + sea based nuclear weapons).

## 3. IT & Communication Technology

### Digital India Programme (2015):
Three core areas:
1. **Digital Infrastructure**: BharatNet (broadband to all gram panchayats), digital identity.
2. **Digital Services**: e-Governance, e-Health, e-Education.
3. **Digital Empowerment**: IT literacy.

### Key Technologies:
- **5G**: Rolled out in India (2022). Uses mm-wave spectrum.
- **UPI (Unified Payments Interface)**: Developed by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India). World's most successful real-time payment system by volume.
- **CoWIN**: COVID-19 vaccine management platform — offered globally.
- **India Stack**: Digital public infrastructure comprising Aadhaar, UPI, DPDP (Data Protection).

### Semiconductors:
- India Semiconductor Mission (2021) — Goal: make India a global chip manufacturing hub.
- **First semiconductor fab in India**: Tata Electronics plant in Dholera (Gujarat) and Assam announced.

## 4. Biotechnology

### Key Institutions:
- **DBT (Department of Biotechnology)**: Nodal government department.
- **BIRAC (Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council)**: Funds biotech startups.
- **National Biotechnology Development Strategy**: India aims to be a top 5 biotech nation.

### Key Applications:
- **Agriculture**: Bt Cotton — only commercially approved GM crop in India. Bt Brinjal approved in Bangladesh. Golden Rice (Vitamin A enriched).
- **Medicine**: Recombinant Insulin (first biotech drug), Monoclonal Antibodies (mAbs), CAR-T cell therapy.
- **COVID Vaccines**: Covaxin (BBV152) — developed by Bharat Biotech with ICMR. Indigenous whole-virion inactivated vaccine.

### GMO Regulation:
- **GEAC (Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee)**: Under MoEFCC. Approves environmental release of GMOs.

## 5. Nanotechnology

- **Nanometre (nm)**: 1 billionth of a metre (10⁻⁹ m).
- **Nanomaterials**: Materials with at least one dimension at 1–100 nm scale.

### Applications:
- **Medicine**: Drug delivery systems, cancer treatment (nanoparticle targeting), nano-biosensors.
- **Defence**: Nano-coatings for stealth aircraft, lightweight armour.
- **Electronics**: Nano-transistors enabling miniaturisation (Moore's Law).
- **Environment**: Nano-filters for water purification, nano-catalysts for pollution control.

### India's Nano Mission:
- **Nano Mission (DST)**: Launched 2007. Funds research, infrastructure, and human resource development in nanotechnology.

## 6. Energy Technology

### Nuclear Energy:
- **DAE (Department of Atomic Energy)**: Apex body for nuclear policy.
- **NPCIL (Nuclear Power Corporation of India)**: Operates all nuclear power plants.
- **3-Stage Nuclear Programme** (conceived by Dr. Homi J. Bhabha):
  - Stage 1: Natural uranium fuel → Pressurised Heavy Water Reactors (PHWR)
  - Stage 2: Plutonium fuel → Fast Breeder Reactors (FBR) — Prototype FBR in Kalpakkam
  - Stage 3: Thorium fuel → India's ultimate aim (world's largest thorium reserves)

### Renewable Energy:
- **India's 2030 Targets**: 500 GW of non-fossil fuel electricity capacity.
- **National Solar Mission**: Part of India's National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC).
- **ISA (International Solar Alliance)**: Founded by India and France (2015, Paris). Headquarters in Gurugram.
