# Constitutional Authorities & Governance
**Covers:** G1 Prelims Unit 4 | G2 Paper 1 Section B
**Sources:** Constitution of India (Part V & VI), NCERT 11 (Executive & Legislature).

---

## 1. The Union Executive
The Union Executive consists of the President, Vice-President, Prime Minister, Council of Ministers, and the Attorney General.

### The President of India (Art 52–62)
-   **Election:** Indirectly elected by an electoral college (Elected members of LS, RS, and State Assemblies).
-   **Powers:** Executive (appoints PM), Legislative (Summons Parliament), Judicial (Pardoning power Art 72), and Emergency powers.
-   **Veto Powers:** Absolute, Suspensive, and Pocket Veto.

### The Prime Minister & Council of Ministers (Art 74–75)
-   **Real Executive Power:** The President acts on the aid and advice of the CoM headed by the PM (Art 74).
-   **Collective Responsibility:** The CoM is collectively responsible to the **Lok Sabha**.

---

## 2. The State Executive
The state executive replicates the union pattern at the state level.

### The Governor (Art 153–161)
-   **Appointment:** Appointed by the President (not elected). Holds office during the pleasure of the President.
-   **Dual Role:** Constitutional head of the state and agent of the Central Government.
-   **Discretionary Powers:** More extensive than the President's (e.g., reserving a bill for the President's consideration).

### The Chief Minister (Art 163–164)
-   **Real Head:** Real executive head of the state level.
-   **Relation to Governor:** Advises the Governor.

---

## 3. The Union Parliament (Art 79–122)
Parliament consists of the President, Rajya Sabha, and Lok Sabha.

-   **Rajya Sabha (Upper House):** Permanent body; 250 members max; 1/3rd members retire every two years.
-   **Lok Sabha (Lower House):** Directly elected; 5-year term; 550 members max.
-   **Speaker of LS:** Presides over the house; decides if a bill is a **Money Bill**.

---

## 4. State Legislature (Art 168–212)
-   **Bicameralism:** Some states have two houses (Legislative Council/Vidhan Parishad and Legislative Assembly/Vidhan Sabha).
-   **Andhra Pradesh:** Re-introduced its Legislative Council in 2007.

---

## 5. Local Government (73rd & 74th Amendments)
-   **73rd Amendment (1992):** Added Part IX (Panchayats) and 11th Schedule.
-   **74th Amendment (1992):** Added Part IXA (Municipalities) and 12th Schedule.
-   **Reservation:** 1/3rd seats reserved for women (Note: Many states like AP have increased this to 50%).
