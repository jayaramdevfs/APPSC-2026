## 1. Historical Background (Evolution)
The Indian Constitution did not emerge in a vacuum; it was the result of long-standing constitutional experiments during British rule.

### The Company Rule (1773–1858)
-   **Regulating Act of 1773:** First step by the British Government to regulate the East India Company. Designated the Governor of Bengal as the **'Governor-General of Bengal'** (Warren Hastings).
-   **Pitt’s India Act of 1784:** Distinguished between commercial and political functions of the Company. Established a **Board of Control**.
-   **Charter Act of 1833:** Made the Governor-General of Bengal the **Governor-General of India** (Lord William Bentinck). It was the final step towards centralization.
-   **Charter Act of 1853:** Separated the legislative and executive functions of the Governor-General’s council.

### The Crown Rule (1858–1947)
-   **Government of India Act of 1858:** Transferred power from the Company to the British Crown. Created the office of the **Secretary of State for India**.
-   **Indian Councils Act of 1909 (Morley-Minto Reforms):** Introduced **separate electorates** for Muslims (Seed of communalism).
-   **Government of India Act of 1919 (Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms):** Introduced **Dyarchy** in provinces.
-   **Government of India Act of 1935:** Provided for an All-India Federation and Dyarchy at the Centre. This Act served as the **major source** for the current Constitution.
-   **Indian Independence Act of 1947:** Declared India as an independent and sovereign state.

## 2. Framing of the Constitution
-   **Constituent Assembly:** Formed in 1946 under the **Cabinet Mission Plan**.
-   **First Meeting:** December 9, 1946 (Sachchidananda Sinha as interim President).
-   **Objective Resolution:** Moved by **Jawaharlal Nehru** on Dec 13, 1946 (became the basis for the Preamble).
-   **Drafting Committee:** Chaired by **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (The Father of the Indian Constitution).
-   **Adoption:** November 26, 1949.
-   **Enforcement:** January 26, 1950 (Republic Day).

## 3. Salient Features of the Constitution
1.  **Longest Written Constitution:** Due to geographical factors and historical diversity.
2.  **Drawn from Various Sources:** 
    *   **UK:** Parliamentary government, Rule of Law, Single citizenship.
    *   **USA:** Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, Preamble.
    *   **Ireland:** Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP).
    *   **Canada:** Federation with a strong Centre.
3.  **Blend of Rigidity and Flexibility:** Some parts require special majority (Art 368), others simple majority.
4.  **Federal System with Unitary Bias:** Described as "Quasi-federal" (K.C. Wheare).
5.  **Parliamentary Form of Government.**
6.  **Independent Judiciary.**
7.  **Secular State.**
8.  **Universal Adult Franchise.**
9.  **Single Citizenship.**

---

## 4. The Preamble
The Preamble is the "Identity Card" of the Constitution (N.A. Palkhivala).
-   **Keywords:** Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic.
-   **Values:** Justice (Social, Economic, Political), Liberty, Equality, Fraternity.
-   **42nd Amendment (1976):** Added words **Socialist, Secular, and Integrity**.
