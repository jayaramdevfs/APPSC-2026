# Current Affairs — APPSC Study Framework

## How to Use This Section
The **Current Affairs** section covers events from the **last 6–12 months** relevant to APPSC Group 1 and Group 2 exams. Navigate using the calendar — each week's digest covers National, International, AP-specific, Economy, and Science & Technology news.

---

## What APPSC Asks from Current Affairs

### Group 1 Prelims (Paper 1 — 150 marks)
- ~20–25 questions on current events (last 12 months)
- Focus: Government schemes, appointments, awards, science missions, AP events
- Format: Factual recall — "Which state/scheme/person/date?"

### Group 2 Screening (150 marks)
- ~15–20 questions on current events
- Focus: National schemes, AP government policies, sports, prizes, summits
- Format: Simple factual; avoid overthinking

### G1 Mains Paper 2 (History + CA)
- Essay-type questions on recent AP government decisions
- Link current events to historical/political context

### 3. Awards & Recognition
**What to note:** Award name → Winner → Field → Year → First-ever/notable angle

**Key awards to track:**

### 5. Andhra Pradesh — High Priority Topics
APPSC consistently tests AP-specific current affairs. Track:

**Economy & Development**
- GDP and GSDP updates, GFCF rankings
- Investment summits (AP Global Investors Summit)
- Polavaram Project: spillway gates, irrigation target, fund releases
- Amaravati Capital City: construction updates, SC/HC orders
- AP Budget announcements (Welfare vs Infrastructure split)

**Governance**
- New schemes by TDP-Jana Sena government (post June 2024)
- Discontinuation of YSR schemes / renamed schemes
- AP Reorganisation Act 2014 — outstanding issues (water, revenue)

**Social**
- AP population data (Census 2021 expected release)
- AP literacy, sex ratio, HDI updates
- Andhra Pradesh State Re-organisation Day (November 1)

## Quick Revision Bullets

- CA questions in APPSC focus on **last 12 months** primarily; last 6 months most heavily tested
- **AP events carry extra weightage** in Group 1 and Group 2 compared to other state PSCs
- Always note the **"APPSC Angle"** section in each weekly digest — it lists the exam-relevant takeaway
- Schemes: Remember **full form, launch year, nodal ministry, and target**
- Summits: Remember **host country/city, theme, India's position/MoUs signed**
- For appointments: note the **appointing authority** (President, Cabinet, collegium, etc.)

---

## What NOT to waste time on
- Day-to-day political party news (winners of no-confidence votes, coalition gossip)
- Stock market numbers
- Cricket scores (unless ICC tournament wins)
- Detailed foreign bilateral trade numbers

Focus on: **policy announcements, institutional changes, AP development, science missions, and national firsts.**
