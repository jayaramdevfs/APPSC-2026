# Defence & Strategic Technologies

## 1. India's Defence Policy Framework

### Key Institutions:
- **Ministry of Defence (MoD)**: Apex body for defence policy.
- **Chief of Defence Staff (CDS)**: Created in January 2020 following the Kargil Review Committee recommendation. First CDS: General Bipin Rawat.
- **Department of Military Affairs (DMA)**: Under the CDS; manages inter-service coordination.
- **DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation)**: Defence R&D. HQ: New Delhi. ~50+ labs across India.

### Tri-Services Commands:
- **Andaman & Nicobar Command**: Only tri-services command in India. Established 2001.
- **Strategic Forces Command**: Controls India's nuclear weapons.

## 2. Nuclear Technology

### India's Nuclear Programme:
- **Homi J. Bhabha**: Father of India's nuclear programme. Founded BARC (then AEET) in 1954.
- **APJ Abdul Kalam**: Integral to Pokhran-I (1974) preparations and led the missile programme; called "Missile Man of India."

### Key Tests:
| Test | Year | Codename | Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pokhran-I** | 1974 | Smiling Buddha / Operation Shakti | First nuclear test; India became 6th nuclear state |
| **Pokhran-II** | 1998 | Operation Shakti | 5 tests; India declared a nuclear weapon state |

### Nuclear Doctrine:
- **No First Use (NFU)**: India will not use nuclear weapons first.
- **Massive Retaliation**: Any nuclear attack on India will be met with massive nuclear retaliation.
- **Civilian Command**: India's nuclear weapons are under civilian control (Political Council of Nuclear Command Authority chaired by PM).

### Nuclear Triad:
India is one of few countries to have a complete nuclear triad:
- **Land**: Agni missile series (Agni-V has ICBM range of 5,000+ km).
- **Air**: Mirage 2000H, Jaguar DARIN — nuclear-capable aircraft.
- **Sea**: INS Arihant — India's first domestically built nuclear submarine (SSBN) — inducted 2016.

## 3. Missile Technology

### Key Missiles:

| Missile | Type | Range | Key Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BrahMos** | Cruise (supersonic) | 300–500 km | Jointly developed with Russia. ~2.8 Mach. |
| **Agni-V** | Ballistic (ICBM class) | 5,500+ km | Multiple independently targetable warheads (MIRV-capable). |
| **Prithvi** | Short-range ballistic | 150–250 km | Surface-to-surface; first indigenously built. |
| **Nag** | Anti-tank guided | 4–7 km | "Fire and forget" ATGM. |
| **Akash** | Surface-to-air (SAM) | 25 km | Air defence; replaces Russian SAMs. |
| **K-15 (Sagarika)** | Submarine-launched | ~750 km | SLBM from INS Arihant. |
| **Shaurya** | Hypersonic | ~750 km | Canister-launched; equivalent to K-15 for land. |

> 💡 **EXAM TRAP**: BrahMos is a **cruise** missile (travels at low altitude, air-breathing engine). Agni is a **ballistic** missile (travels through space in an arc). Different flight profiles.

## 4. Space Defence (ASAT & Military Satellites)

### ASAT (Anti-Satellite) Weapons:
- **Mission Shakti (2019)**: India became only the 4th country (after USA, Russia, China) to successfully test an ASAT weapon. Shot down a live satellite in Low Earth Orbit using a Kinetic Kill Vehicle.
- **DSRO → SpaceX equivalent**: ISRO's commercial arm **IN-SPACe** and **NewSpace India Ltd.** manage dual-use satellites.

### Key Military Satellites:
- **GSAT series**: Communication satellites (some dedicated for military/secure comms).
- **RISAT (Radar Imaging Satellite)**: All-weather, day-night surveillance.
- **EMISAT**: Electronic intelligence satellite for detecting enemy radar.
- **Cartosat**: High-resolution earth observation; used for border surveillance.

## 5. Cyber Security

- **CERT-In (Computer Emergency Response Team – India)**: National nodal agency for cyber incident response. Under MeitY.
- **NCIIPC (National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre)**: Under NTRO. Protects critical infrastructure.
- **India's Cyber Security Strategy**: Multi-layered framework covering government, private sector, and citizens.
- **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**: India's first comprehensive data protection law. Establishes Data Protection Board.
- **Common Threats**: Phishing, ransomware, DDoS attacks, social engineering, state-sponsored attacks.

## 6. Indigenisation & Defence Manufacturing

### Atmanirbhar Bharat in Defence:
- **Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020**: Prioritises indigenous procurement (Buy Indian — IDDM first preference).
- **Negative Import Lists**: Items that cannot be imported; must be procured domestically. 3 such lists issued.
- **Defence Industrial Corridors**:
  - **Uttar Pradesh Defence Corridor**: Lucknow–Agra axis.
  - **Tamil Nadu Defence Corridor**: Chennai–Coimbatore axis.

### Key Indigenous Projects:
| System | Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HAL Tejas (LCA)** | Inducted into IAF; Mk-2 version under development. |
| **INS Vikrant (IAC-1)** | India's first indigenous aircraft carrier; commissioned 2022. |
| **INSAS Rifle** | Standard infantry rifle; being replaced by SiG Sauer M400. |
| **FICV (Future Infantry Combat Vehicle)** | Under development to replace BMP-2 IFVs. |
| **Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA)** | 5th generation stealth fighter; design finalized. |
