# Andhra Pradesh Economy

## 1. Overview of AP Economy

- **GSDP (Gross State Domestic Product)**: AP is among the top 10 states by GSDP in India.
- **Economy Type**: Largely agrarian with a rapidly growing services sector.
- **Per Capita Income**: Above the national average after bifurcation and reconstruction efforts.
- **Capital Cities**: Amaravati (Capital City), Visakhapatnam (Executive Capital), Kurnool (Judicial Capital) — The **Three Capital Policy** under the YSRCP government (2020 onwards).

> 💡 **EXAM TRAP**: As of 2024, AP operates with a **three-capital structure** (legislative at Amaravati, executive at Vizag, judicial at Kurnool) — not a settled matter politically.

## 2. Agriculture in Andhra Pradesh

### Key Crops:
| Crop | Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rice** | Major Kharif crop; AP is one of the top producers |
| **Sugarcane** | Mainly in Krishna, West Godavari, East Godavari |
| **Cotton** | Major cash crop; rainfed; Guntur, Kurnool, Kadapa |
| **Tobacco** | AP is the **largest producer** in India; Guntur is the hub |
| **Chilli** | Guntur is the **world's largest chilli market** |
| **Aquaculture** | AP is **No. 1 in India** for shrimp/prawn production; West Godavari, Krishna districts |

### Irrigation in AP:
- **Major Irrigation Projects**: Nagarjuna Sagar, Srisailam, Pulichintala, Polavaram (under construction).
- **Polavaram Project**: National Project; multipurpose dam on River Godavari. Will irrigate 2.91 lakh acres; generate 960 MW hydropower. Crucial for AP water security.
- **Tank Irrigation**: AP has the highest density of tanks in India (~100,000 tanks).

## 3. Industries in Andhra Pradesh

### Key Industrial Hubs:
| Location | Industries |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Visakhapatnam** | Steel (RINL-VSP), Petroleum (HPCL refinery), Shipbuilding, Port |
| **Tirupati** | Auto components, Leather goods, Textiles |
| **Guntur** | Agro-processing, Tobacco, Cotton textiles |
| **Sri City (Chittoor)** | Multi-product SEZ; MNCs like Kellogg's, Isuzu |
| **APSEZ (Krishnapatnam)** | Major private port + industrial zone |
| **Kadapa** | Cement (AP is rich in limestone), Uranium (Tummalapalle mine) |

### Key PSUs in AP:
- **RINL (Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Ltd.)** — "Vizag Steel" — India's only Shore-Based Integrated Steel Plant.
- **HPCL-Vizag Refinery**: Major petroleum refinery.
- **APGENCO** and **APTRANSCO**: Power generation and transmission.

## 4. AP's Vision & Key Policies

### Vision AP 2047:
- AP aims to become a **$2.4 trillion economy** by 2047 (Centenary of India's Independence).
- Focus areas: IT/ITeS, Aquaculture, Agriculture-value addition, Renewable Energy.

### Key Economic Policies:
- **AP Industrial Development Policy**: Single-window clearance for investors; incentive-based attraction.
- **AP Sunrise** initiative: Global investment summits to attract FDI.
- **MSME Policy**: Special clusters for textiles (Ongole), leather (Nellore), marine products.

## 5. Infrastructure & Connectivity

### Ports:
- **Visakhapatnam Port Trust (VPT)**: Major Port — one of India's largest by cargo volume; handles coal, crude oil, iron ore.
- **Krishnapatnam Port (APSEZ)**: Largest private port on India's east coast.
- **Gangavaram Port**: Merged with Adani Ports; handles coal, fertilisers.
- **Kakinada Port**: Handles petroleum products and fertilisers; special economic zone nearby.

### Power Sector:
- AP has aggressive renewable energy targets.
- **Solar**: Large installations in Kurnool (Kurnool Ultra Mega Solar Park — 1,000 MW — one of the world's largest single-location solar parks when commissioned).
- **Wind**: Coastal AP (Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Krishna) has high wind potential.

## 6. AP State Finances

### Revenue Sources:
1. **Own Tax Revenue**: Commercial Taxes (GST state share), Stamps & Registration, Excise, Motor Vehicle Tax.
2. **Central Transfers**: Tax devolution (41% share from central taxes) + Grants-in-aid.
3. **Borrowings**: Capital market loans, external loans.

### AP's Fiscal Challenges Post-Bifurcation:
- AP lost Hyderabad (a major revenue generator) to Telangana.
- Central government committed to a special financial package under AP Reorganisation Act, 2014.
- The state has faced persistent revenue deficits and high debt-to-GSDP ratios.

### AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 — Key Promises:
- Special Category Status (debated; not fully granted).
- Polavaram project as National Project.
- Development package for backward regions.
- Rail, road, and power sector investments.

## 7. Human Development in AP

| Indicator | AP Performance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Literacy Rate | ~67% (Census 2011) — below national average of 73% |
| Sex Ratio | 993 per 1000 males (above national avg. of 940) |
| IMR (Infant Mortality Rate) | Declining; below national average |
| Access to Power | Near-universal electrification |

### DWCRA (Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas):
- State-level scheme promoting self-help groups (SHGs) — AP became a model for SHG-bank linkage programmes that influenced the national NRLM.

### Amma Vodi, Jagananna Gorumudda etc.:
- AP's flagship welfare schemes under the YSRCP government (2019-2024) targeted education, nutrition, and direct benefit transfers to disadvantaged groups.
