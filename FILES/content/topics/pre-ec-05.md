# Andhra Pradesh Economy

> *AP lost its capital city, its largest taxpayer, and half its economy in 2014. What it has done since is one of India's most dramatic state-level economic rebuilding stories — and it is far from finished.*

**Covers:** G2 Screening | G1 Prelims AP Economy | G1 Mains Paper 3 | G2 Paper 2 & 3
**Sources:** AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024–25, AP Reorganisation Act 2014, NABARD AP Reports

---

## The Big Picture

When Andhra Pradesh was bifurcated in June 2014, it lost Hyderabad — the financial heart of the united state, contributing over 50% of its tax revenue — to the new state of Telangana. AP was left with a massive development gap: no capital city (Amaravati was a greenfield project), no major industrial hub comparable to Hyderabad, a revenue deficit, and the challenge of building state capacity from scratch.

Yet AP has demonstrated remarkable resilience. Its agricultural sector remained intact (the Krishna-Godavari delta, India's rice bowl). Its coastline is the third longest in India. Its ports are strategic assets. And the state set about attracting investment with a ferocity that earned it India's **#1 ranking in Ease of Doing Business** for multiple years.

For APPSC candidates, AP economy questions are among the most frequently asked and the most AP-specific. The exact numbers — GSDP growth, agricultural outputs, industrial rankings — change year to year but the structural features remain constant. Know the structure; learn the latest numbers from the AP Socio-Economic Survey.

**Capital Structure (Post-2024):**
- AP operates with the **three-capital arrangement** (Amaravati, Visakhapatnam, Kurnool as legislative, executive, and judicial capitals respectively) — though the political situation on this was contested. The current TDP-led government's position (2024–) may differ.

> 📌 **Exam Note:** The three-capital policy was the YSRCP government's (2019–24) approach. The previous TDP and current TDP governments favored Amaravati as the single capital. APPSC may test either the policy or the current status — check the most recent AP Government notification closer to the exam.

## 2. Agriculture in Andhra Pradesh

AP's agricultural strength is one of the most tested areas in APPSC. Know the crops, irrigation systems, and specific district-level facts:

### Key Crops of AP

| Crop | Districts | AP's National Rank | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paddy (Rice)** | Krishna, West Godavari, East Godavari, Guntur | Top 5 states | Main Kharif crop; 127.15 lakh tonnes (2024–25) |
| **Sugarcane** | Krishna, West Godavari, East Godavari | Significant producer | Major agro-industrial crop |
| **Cotton** | Guntur, Kurnool, Kadapa, Anantapur | Major producer | Rainfed; black soil areas |
| **Tobacco** | Guntur | **Largest in India** | Guntur = national tobacco hub; controversial export crop |
| **Chilli** | **Guntur** | **World's largest chilli market** | Guntur Sannam is globally traded variety |
| **Horticulture (overall)** | State-wide | **1st in India** (15.6% share) | Fruits, vegetables, flowers |
| **Mangoes** | Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Krishna | **2nd in India** | Banganapalle variety (GI tag) |
| **Aquaculture/Shrimp** | **West Godavari, Krishna, East Godavari, Nellore** | **1st in India** | AP = 30% of national fish output; 31% shrimp exports |

> 🔑 **Key Insight:** Guntur district is AP's agricultural powerhouse — it is simultaneously the world's largest chilli market AND India's largest tobacco hub. The Guntur Sannam chilli variety is exported globally. Guntur is also significant for cotton. All three crops grow on the same black/red soil base and are deeply connected to AP's agro-industrial economy.

### Irrigation in AP

| System | Coverage | Key Projects |
|---|---|---|
| **Tank Irrigation** | ~1 lakh tanks — highest tank density in India | Traditional water harvesting; monsoon-fed |
| **Canal Irrigation** | Delta districts (Krishna, Godavari) | Nagarjunasagar Dam, Krishna Barrage |
| **Major Projects** | — | — |

**Polavaram Project (National Project):**
- Location: Godavari River (West Godavari district)
- Status: **~78% complete** (2024–25 figure)
- Declared: National Project under Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014
- Irrigation benefit: **2.91 lakh hectares** (7.2 lakh acres) in Krishna, West Godavari, East Godavari
- Power generation: **960 MW** hydropower
- Drinking water: Supply to Visakhapatnam city
- Tribal displacement: ~1.1 lakh families from Agency areas to be rehabilitated

**Other Major Dams/Projects:**
- **Nagarjunasagar Dam**: Godavari-Krishna linking; one of the world's largest masonry dams
- **Srisailam Dam**: On Krishna River; hydropower + irrigation; in Nallamala Hills
- **Pulichintala Project**: On Krishna River; supplementary irrigation

## 3. Industries in Andhra Pradesh

### Key Industrial Hubs and Their Specializations

| City/District | Key Industries |
|---|---|
| **Visakhapatnam** | Steel (RINL-VSP), Petroleum (HPCL Refinery), Shipbuilding (Hindustan Shipyard), Defence (DRDO, Submarine base), Port |
| **Tirupati** | Auto components (Toyota supply chain), Leather goods, Electronics |
| **Guntur** | Agro-processing, Tobacco processing, Cotton textiles |
| **Sri City (Chittoor)** | Multi-product SEZ; MNCs (Kellogg's, Isuzu, Kobelco); considered one of Asia's best industrial parks |
| **Krishnapatnam (Nellore)** | Major private port (APSEZ) + industrial zone; CBIC corridor node |
| **Kadapa** | Cement (AP is rich in limestone), Uranium (Tummalapalle mine — India's largest uranium deposit) |
| **Kakinada** | LNG imports, Petroleum products; KG Basin proximity |

### Key PSUs in AP

| PSU | Location | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **RINL (Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Ltd.)** | Visakhapatnam | India's **only shore-based integrated steel plant**; uses imported raw materials via sea |
| **HPCL-Vizag Refinery** | Visakhapatnam | Major petroleum refinery; coastal proximity advantage |
| **APGENCO** | State-wide | AP's government power generation company |
| **APTRANSCO** | State-wide | AP's power transmission company |

### Industrial Policy and Investment Attraction

- **Ease of Doing Business**: AP ranks **#1 in India** — a flagship achievement and high-frequency APPSC fact
- **Industrial Development Policy**: Single-window clearance for investors; land allocation, utility connections in defined timeframes
- **GSDP Share of Manufacturing**: ~11%
- **Three Industrial Corridors** in AP:
  - **VCIC** (Vizag-Chennai Industrial Corridor): AP's flagship; ports, logistics, manufacturing along east coast
  - **CBIC** (Chennai-Bengaluru): Krishnapatnam (Nellore) is the AP node
  - **HBIC** (Hyderabad-Bengaluru): Orvakal (Kurnool) node

> 📌 **Exam Note:** **Tummalapalle** in YSR Kadapa district has **India's largest uranium ore deposit**. Uranium Corporation of India Ltd. (UCIL) operates here. This is significant for India's nuclear energy program — and a high-frequency AP geography + economy fact.

## 4. Ports and Infrastructure

### AP's Ports

| Port | District | Type | Key Cargo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visakhapatnam Port (VPT)** | Visakhapatnam | **Major Port** (12 Major Ports of India) | Coal, crude oil, iron ore, containers; **deepest landlocked protected harbor** |
| **Krishnapatnam Port (APSEZ)** | Nellore | Non-major (private) | India's **largest private port** on east coast; containers, dry bulk |
| **Gangavaram Port** | Visakhapatnam | Non-major | Coal, fertilizers; merged with Adani Ports |
| **Kakinada Port** | East Godavari | Non-major | LNG imports, petroleum; KG Basin proximity |
| **Machilipatnam Port** (developing) | Krishna | Planned | Part of VCIC |

### Power Sector

- AP has aggressive renewable energy targets aligned with India's 500 GW non-fossil target by 2030
- **Kurnool Ultra Mega Solar Park**: 1,000 MW — one of the world's largest single-location solar parks when commissioned
- **Wind Energy**: Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, and Krishna coastal districts have high potential
- **DRDO & Defence**: AP hosts major defence research and production facilities at Visakhapatnam (Naval base, submarine construction yard)

## 5. AP State Finances

### Revenue Sources

1. **Own Tax Revenue**: Commercial Taxes (GST state share), Stamps & Registration Fees, Excise Duty, Motor Vehicle Tax
2. **Central Transfers**: Tax devolution (41% share from central pool per 15th Finance Commission) + Grants-in-aid
3. **Borrowings**: Market loans, external loans (World Bank, JICA), NABARD refinance

### AP's Fiscal Challenges Post-Bifurcation

The bifurcation created a structural fiscal problem that AP is still working through:
- Lost **Hyderabad** (50%+ of tax revenue) to Telangana
- New capital Amaravati requires massive fresh investment
- High debt-to-GSDP ratio post-bifurcation
- Revenue deficit for several years post-2014

### AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 — Key Promises (and Status)

| Promise | Status |
|---|---|
| **Special Category Status** | Not formally granted (Centre's position: SCS criteria changed); ongoing political demand |
| **Polavaram as National Project** | Granted; funded by Centre |
| **Backward areas development** | Partial: Andhra Pradesh Development Package |
| **Rail, road, power investments** | Partially delivered; Visakhapatnam Metro approved |

## 6. Human Development in AP

| Indicator | AP Performance |
|---|---|
| **Literacy Rate** | ~67.35% (Census 2011) — **below national average of 74%** |
| **Sex Ratio** | **997** per 1,000 males — above national average of 943 |
| **IMR** (Infant Mortality Rate) | Below national average (improved significantly) |
| **Power access** | Near-universal electrification |

**AP's SHG (Self-Help Group) Model:**
- **DWCRA (Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas)**: AP was the national pioneer of the SHG-bank linkage model
- AP's SHG movement (now under SERP — Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty) is one of the world's largest — ~90 lakh women members
- This model was scaled nationally as **NRLM (National Rural Livelihoods Mission)**

> 🔑 **Key Insight:** AP's SHG movement is a globally studied example of financial inclusion and women's empowerment. The **SHG-bank linkage model** — where self-help groups are linked to formal banks for credit — was pioneered in AP's DWCRA program and scaled nationally. AP has the largest SHG network in the world.
