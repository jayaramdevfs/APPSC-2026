# Infrastructure in India & Andhra Pradesh

> *India's infrastructure story is one number: 1991. Before liberalization, the state built everything and built it slowly. After 1991, private capital entered — and India went from 230 km/year of new national highways to 10,000 km/year in three decades. But the gap between capacity and need remains enormous.*

**Covers:** G1 Mains Paper 4 (Economy) | G1 Prelims Economy | G2 Screening Economy
**Sources:** Economic Survey of India; AP Economic Survey; NITI Aayog Reports; Ministry of Road Transport

## The Big Picture

Infrastructure is the foundation of economic growth — no factory, hospital, or school reaches its potential without roads, power, water, and connectivity. For India, the infrastructure deficit (the gap between what exists and what is needed) has historically been one of the largest constraints on growth. The story of Indian infrastructure post-1991 is one of gradual acceleration — PPP models, NHDP, dedicated freight corridors, the National Infrastructure Pipeline — interrupted by land acquisition bottlenecks, power sector NPAs, and the challenge of financing ₹100 lakh crore investments.

For APPSC, the AP-specific facts are critical: NH-16, Visakhapatnam Port, Kakinada Port, Polavaram irrigation project, AP Power distribution companies, AP's Industrial Corridors, and the APSEZ. Master the tables; the concepts explain what the tables mean.

## 1. Roads and Highways

### Road Network Classification

| Category | Authority | Characteristics |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **National Highways (NH)** | NHAI / MoRTH | Connect major cities and state capitals; all-weather roads |
| **State Highways (SH)** | State PWD | Connect district headquarters; maintained by state |
| **Major District Roads (MDR)** | State PWD | Connect taluks to district HQ |
| **Rural Roads (Gram Panchayat Roads)** | PMGSY | Last-mile connectivity; all-weather rural roads |

**India's road network:** ~63 lakh km total — 2nd largest in the world; National Highways = ~1.45 lakh km

### National Highways Development Programme (NHDP)

The **National Highways Authority of India (NHAI)** was established under the NHAI Act 1988. The ambitious road-building programme accelerated under NDA governments:

| Phase | Programme | Route/Target |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Golden Quadrilateral (GQ)** | Connect Delhi–Mumbai–Chennai–Kolkata | ~5,846 km; 4/6 lane; largely complete |
| **North-South & East-West Corridor** | Srinagar–Kanyakumari + Porbandar–Silchar | ~7,142 km |
| **Bharatmala Pariyojana** | 2017 onwards | 34,800 km; economic corridors + border roads + coastal roads |

> 🔑 **Key Insight:** **Bharatmala Pariyojana (2017)** is the largest highway construction programme in India's history — targeting 34,800 km across 44 Economic Corridors, 6 Ring Roads around metro cities, and inter-corridors. It absorbs and extends all previous NHDP phases. Under Bharatmala, India's highway construction rate crossed **10,000 km/year** by 2021 — compared to ~2,000 km/year in the early 2000s.

### National Highways in Andhra Pradesh

| Highway | Route | Significance |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| **NH-16** | Kolkata–Chennai (via Vijayawada–Nellore) | **Main spine of AP** — passes through Visakhapatnam, Rajahmundry, Vijayawada, Ongole, Nellore |
| **NH-65** | Pune–Vijayawada | Connects Deccan to AP; important for trade |
| **NH-167** | Visakhapatnam–Raipur | Connects AP to Chhattisgarh; steel plant logistics |
| **NH-40** (old NH-9) | Hyderabad direction connectivity | Residual AP connectivity to Hyderabad |

> 📌 **Exam Note:** **NH-16** (formerly NH-5, old National Highway 5) is the most important national highway for AP — it connects Kolkata to Chennai passing through virtually all of coastal AP's major cities. This is the commercial lifeline of AP.

## 2. Railways

### Zonal Railways in India

India has **18 railway zones** (as of 2019). South-Central India is covered by:

| Zone | Headquarters | AP Coverage |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **South Coast Railway (SCR)** | Visakhapatnam | New zone carved out in 2019; covers Visakhapatnam–Rayagada, Vijayawada |
| **South Central Railway (SCR)** | Secunderabad (Hyderabad) | Covers parts of AP connecting to Telangana |
| **East Coast Railway (ECoR)** | Bhubaneswar | Covers Visakhapatnam–Bhubaneswar corridor |

> 📌 **Exam Note:** **South Coast Railway (SCoR)** was the **18th railway zone**, created in **2019** with headquarters at **Visakhapatnam**. This was specifically carved out for the benefit of residual AP after bifurcation. Before this, AP had no railway zone headquarters of its own. High-frequency APPSC fact.

### Key Railway Projects in AP

| Project | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC)** | Eastern DFC (Delhi–Kolkata) and Western DFC (Delhi–Mumbai); AP is on the Eastern approach |
| **Visakhapatnam–Raipur line** | Critical for steel, coal, and mineral transport from AP to Chhattisgarh |
| **Vizag Metro Rail** | Urban metro for Visakhapatnam — under planning/development |
| **Amaravati Rail connectivity** | Rail link to new capital being planned |

## 3. Ports — Maritime Infrastructure

India has **12 Major Ports** (under central government) and ~200 **Non-Major (Minor) Ports** (under state governments).

### Ports in Andhra Pradesh

| Port | Status | Significance |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Visakhapatnam Port** | **Major Port** | India's deepest natural port; handles iron ore, coal, crude oil; one of busiest major ports |
| **Krishnapatnam Port** | Non-Major → **upgraded** | Private port; handles containers, coal; major capacity growth |
| **Kakinada Port** | Non-Major | Oil sector; ONGC related; deepwater facility |
| **Gangavaram Port** | Non-Major | Private; bulk cargo; coal and fertilizers |
| **Machilipatnam Port** | Development phase | Historical port; being developed under new investment |

> 🔑 **Key Insight:** **Visakhapatnam Port** is the only **Major Port** in AP — governed by central government. All others are state/private ports. Visakhapatnam is called India's **deepest landlocked port** — natural deep harbor allows very large vessels. It handles ~70 million tonnes per year. The **Sagarmala scheme** (2015) is developing port-led industrialization around all major ports including Visakhapatnam.

### Sagarmala Scheme (2015)

- **Objective**: Port-led development — modernize ports, enhance port connectivity, promote port-led industrialization
- **Components**: Port modernization + New port development + Port connectivity enhancement + Port-linked industrialization
- **AP benefit**: Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, Krishnapatnam all under Sagarmala for upgrades

## 4. Power Sector in Andhra Pradesh

### National Power Overview

India's installed power capacity (2023): ~**420 GW** — one of the world's largest

| Source | Capacity (approx.) | Share |
|--------|---------------------|-------|
| Thermal (coal, gas, oil) | ~235 GW | ~56% |
| Renewable (solar, wind, hydro, biomass) | ~175 GW | ~42% |
| Nuclear | ~7 GW | ~2% |

**AP Power Structure (post-2014 bifurcation):**

| Organization | Function |
|-------------|---------|
| **APGENCO** (AP Power Generation Corporation) | Generation — thermal, hydro, gas power plants |
| **APTRANSCO** (AP Transmission Corporation) | Transmission — high-voltage grid |
| **APEPDCL** (AP Eastern Power Distribution Company) | Distribution — north coastal AP, Visakhapatnam, Rajahmundry |
| **APSPDCL** (AP Southern Power Distribution Company) | Distribution — south AP, Vijayawada, Nellore, Kurnool, Tirupati |

> 📌 **Exam Note:** AP has **one Transco** but **two Discoms**. APEPDCL covers the northern/eastern districts; APSPDCL covers the southern/western districts. This bifurcation of distribution is specific to AP and differs from most states. Both distribute electricity purchased from APGENCO and central allocations.

### AP Power Projects

| Project | Type | Capacity | Location |
|---------|------|----------|----------|
| **Narla Tata Rao Thermal Power Station** | Thermal | 1,760 MW | Ibrahimpatnam, Krishna |
| **Krishnapatnam Thermal** | Thermal | 1,600 MW | SPML project |
| **Sri Damodaram Sanjeevaiah Thermal** | Thermal | 1,600 MW | Krishnapatnam |
| **Srisailam Hydroelectric** | Hydro | 770 MW | Srisailam (shared with Telangana) |
| **Polavaram Hydroelectric** | Hydro | **960 MW** | West Godavari (under construction) |
| **Kurnool Ultra Mega Solar Park** | Solar | **1,000 MW** | Kurnool; one of world's largest solar parks |

## 5. Irrigation — Polavaram Project

The **Polavaram Project** is the most important infrastructure project for AP:

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| River | **Godavari** (Polavaram reservoir across Godavari) |
| Location | **West Godavari district** (Polavaram village area) |
| Status | **National Project** — declared under AP Reorganisation Act 2014 |
| Ayacut (irrigation) | **7.2 lakh acres** of new farmland in Andhra and Odisha |
| Drinking water | Drinking water to **23 districts** of AP |
| Hydropower | **960 MW** |
| Interlinking | Will transfer water from Godavari to Krishna basin; link to drought-prone Rayalaseema |
| Displaced | Over 1 lakh tribal families from Polavaram area (rehabilitation under dispute) |

> 🔑 **Key Insight:** **Polavaram = AP's lifeline project**. It is the **only project that can solve AP's chronic water deficit** in Rayalaseema and southern districts. Polavaram redirects surplus Godavari water (which flows unused to sea) into Krishna basin and then to drought zones. Being a National Project means Centre funds it — AP's biggest post-bifurcation relief. Completion has been delayed repeatedly; CM-to-CM political football since 2014.

## 6. Industrial Corridors & Special Economic Zones

### AP Industrial Corridors

| Corridor | Route | Focus Industries |
|----------|-------|-----------------|
| **Vizag–Chennai Industrial Corridor (VCIC)** | Visakhapatnam to Chennai (600 km coast) | Manufacturing, ports, petrochemicals |
| **Chennai–Bengaluru Industrial Corridor (CBIC)** | Renigunta (AP portion) to Bengaluru | Automobiles, electronics, aerospace |

### APSEZ — Andhra Pradesh Special Economic Zone

- **Location**: Atchutapuram, Visakhapatnam district
- **Type**: Multi-sector SEZ — manufacturing, IT, pharma, food processing
- **Operator**: AP Industrial Infrastructure Corporation (APIIC)
- **Significance**: Largest SEZ in AP; connected to Visakhapatnam Port

### PCPIR — Petroleum, Chemicals and Petrochemicals Investment Region

- **Location**: **Visakhapatnam–Kakinada** coastal belt (~50 km stretch)
- **Focus**: Oil refineries, gas crackers, chemical plants, downstream industries
- **Anchor**: Hindustan Petroleum's refinery at Visakhapatnam (HPCL Vizag Refinery)

## 7. Digital Infrastructure

### National Digital Initiatives

| Initiative | Detail |
|-----------|--------|

| **Digital India** | Launched 2015; 9 pillars including broadband, digital services, digital literacy |

### AP Fiber Grid

- **AP-FiberNet (APSFL)**: Government broadband provider in AP — **Andhra Pradesh State Fiber Net Limited**
- Target: Affordable broadband to every household and gram panchayat in AP
- **AP is one of the first states to build a fully owned state fiber network** — government controls the last-mile
