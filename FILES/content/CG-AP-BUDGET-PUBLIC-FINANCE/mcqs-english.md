Q1. As per the First Advance Estimates (FAE) for 2024-25, the Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) of Andhra Pradesh at current prices is estimated at:
(a) ₹12.06 lakh crore
(b) ₹14.22 lakh crore
(c) ₹16.06 lakh crore
(d) ₹18.06 lakh crore

Answer: (c)
Explanation: As per the FAE for 2024-25, AP's GSDP at current prices is estimated at ₹16.06 lakh crore, reflecting a growth rate of 12.94% over the previous year (₹14.22 lakh crore in 2023-24 FRE). Option (b) is the previous year's figure — a common trap.

Q2. Consider the following statements regarding the Per Capita Income of Andhra Pradesh in 2024-25:
1. The Per Capita Income of AP (₹2,68,653) is higher than the All India average.
2. AP's Per Capita Income grew at a rate of 12.9%, which is higher than the national growth rate.
3. AP's Per Capita Income has been consistently lower than the national average since the state's bifurcation.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (a)
Explanation: Statement 1 is correct — AP's PCI (₹2,68,653) exceeds the All India PCI of ₹2,00,162. Statement 2 is correct — AP's PCI growth (12.9%) exceeds the national growth (8.7%). Statement 3 is incorrect — AP's PCI has been consistently HIGHER than the national average since 2014-15, not lower.

Q4. Consider the following matched pairs regarding AP's fiscal indicators for FY 2024-25 (RE):
1. Total Outstanding Debt : ₹5,64,488 Crores
2. Revenue Deficit : ₹73,362 Crores
3. Debt-to-GSDP ratio : 35.15%
4. Interest Payments : ₹32,944 Crores
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1, 2 and 3
(b) 1, 3 and 4
(c) 2 and 4 only
(d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

Answer: (b)
Explanation: Pairs 1, 3, and 4 are correctly matched. Pair 2 is incorrect — ₹73,362 Crores is the FISCAL Deficit, not Revenue Deficit. The Revenue Deficit is ₹48,311 Crores. This is a common exam trap — confusing Revenue Deficit with Fiscal Deficit.

Q8. The fiscal stress of Andhra Pradesh post-bifurcation can be attributed to multiple factors. Which among the following is NOT cited as a reason in the AP Socio-Economic Survey?
(a) Shrinking of revenue base after loss of Hyderabad
(b) Rising establishment costs and interest payments
(c) Excess capital expenditure on new industries exceeding revenue
(d) Lesser than promised flow of funds from Government of India

Answer: (c)
Explanation: The AP Survey cites shrinking revenue base, rising establishment costs, interest payments, welfare commitments, and loss of Hyderabad as causes of fiscal stress. It also mentions that GoI did not fulfil assurances. However, excess capital expenditure on new industries is NOT cited — in fact, capital outlay (₹24,072 Cr) is relatively modest compared to revenue expenditure (₹2,24,343 Cr).

Q10. Andhra Pradesh's Debt-to-GSDP ratio for FY 2024-25 (RE) stood at 35.15%. In the context of India's fiscal management framework, this ratio is significant because:
(a) It is below the 25% limit recommended by the 15th Finance Commission for all states.
(b) It exceeds the 28% limit set by the FRBM Act for state governments.
(c) It is approaching the prudential debt limits, raising concerns about long-term fiscal sustainability.
(d) It is the lowest Debt-to-GSDP ratio among all Indian states according to the Survey.

Answer: (c)
Explanation: AP's Debt-to-GSDP ratio at 35.15% is approaching prudential limits, raising fiscal sustainability concerns. The outstanding debt increased from ₹4,91,734 Crores (2023-24) to ₹5,64,488 Crores (2024-25 RE), and interest payments rose to ₹32,944 Crores. Options (a) and (b) cite incorrect thresholds, and option (d) is not supported by the Survey data. The rising debt and widening deficits indicate fiscal stress.
