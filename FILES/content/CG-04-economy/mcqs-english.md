## Q01
**Question:** The RBI was established on the recommendation of which of the following commissions?
**Options:**
A) Royal Commission on Indian Currency and Finance (Hilton Young Commission)
B) Chamberlain Commission
C) Fowler Commission
D) Babington Smith Commission
**Correct Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The RBI was set up based on the recommendations of the Hilton Young Commission (1926). The RBI Act was passed in 1934, and it commenced operations on April 1, 1935.
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Topic:** MT-ECON-G2P3-3-001 — RBI Functions & Monetary Policy
**Source:** Ramesh Singh (Ch 12)

## Q02
**Question:** Which of the following is NOT a quantitative tool of monetary policy used by the RBI?
**Options:**
A) Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR)
B) Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)
C) Moral Suasion
D) Open Market Operations (OMO)
**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Moral Suasion is a qualitative (selective) tool. CRR, SLR, and OMO are quantitative (general) tools used to regulate the total volume of credit in the economy.
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Topic:** MT-ECON-G2P3-3-001 — RBI Functions & Monetary Policy
**Source:** Ramesh Singh (Ch 12)

## Q04
**Question:** When the RBI reduces the Repo Rate, what is the likely impact on the economy?
**Options:**
A) Borrowing becomes cheaper, leading to increased investment and consumption.
B) Banks increase their lending rates to maintain margins.
C) Money supply in the economy decreases.
D) Inflation is likely to fall.
**Correct Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Repo Rate is the rate at which RBI lends to banks. Reducing it makes funds cheaper for banks, which usually pass on the benefit to consumers, boosting demand.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Topic:** MT-ECON-G2P3-3-001 — RBI Functions & Monetary Policy

## Q08
**Question:** Which of the following is considered as 'FDI' rather than 'FPI' in the Indian context?
**Options:**
A) Purchase of shares in an Indian company by a foreign individual below 10% of equity.
B) Investment by a foreign entity in a project that leads to 10% or more ownership and management control.

**Question:** "Per Capita Income" (PCI) of a country is calculated by dividing its:
**Options:**
A) GDP by its total area.
B) GNP by its total working population.
C) NNP (National Income) by its total population.

## Q26
**Question:** Which of the following statements regarding 'GVA at Basic Prices' is correct?
**Options:**
A) It is equivalent to GVA at Factor Cost plus product taxes.
B) It includes production taxes but excludes product taxes.
C) It is the same as the final market price of the good.
