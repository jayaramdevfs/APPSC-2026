# Current Affairs — Week of September 29, 2025

## International
- **India-US iCET Review**: India and US reviewed progress under the Initiative on Critical and Emerging Technologies (iCET) — semiconductor supply chains, AI governance, and space cooperation updates shared at the annual review meet.
- **WHO Global TB Report 2025**: WHO released the Global Tuberculosis Report — India accounts for 26% of global TB burden but achieved a 17% reduction in incidence since 2015; India's TB Elimination Mission targets 2025 end date (extended to 2027).

## National
- **TB Elimination Mission Update**: India's National TB Elimination Programme (NTEP) achieved 22% reduction in incidence since 2015 but fell short of 2025 elimination target; mission extended to **2027** with enhanced Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana support.
- **India's Forest Area — 9th Globally**: According to FAO's Global Forest Resources Assessment (GFRA) 2025, India ranks **9th globally in total forest area** and **3rd in annual net forest gain** — reflecting success of van mahotsav and compensatory afforestation programs.
- **NISAR Satellite Preparation**: ISRO and NASA finalised the NISAR (NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar) satellite pre-launch checks; launch scheduled for early 2026; will map Earth's surface every 12 days.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Forest Cover**: AP's forest cover increased by 647 sq km in 2023-25 period per FSI data — benefiting from Green AP Mission plantations along river corridors and farm bunds.
- **AP TB Elimination**: AP ranked among the top 5 states in TB case detection rate; launched district-wise TB-free certification drive targeting 5 districts by December 2025.

## Economy & Finance
- **India-New Zealand FTA Negotiations**: India and New Zealand completed the 7th round of FTA negotiations — covering goods, services, dairy (contentious), and investment protection; final agreement expected by December 2025.

## Science & Technology
- **NISAR Satellite**: Joint ISRO-NASA radar satellite; will produce the most detailed maps of Earth's surface changes (land deformation, ecosystem changes, groundwater); 12-day repeat cycle globally; launch in 2026.
