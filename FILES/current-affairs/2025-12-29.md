# Current Affairs — Week of December 29, 2025

## International
- **Year-End Global Economy Review**: IMF's end-year assessment — India's GDP growth 7.1% for FY2025-26 (revised up from 6.8%); China at 4.5%; US at 2.4%; India confirmed as world's fastest-growing major economy.
- **India-Bangladesh Normalisation Talks**: India and Bangladesh resumed diplomatic back-channel talks to stabilise relations after political transition; India offered to resume power supply agreements and Teesta water sharing discussions.
