# Current Affairs — Week of October 20, 2025

## International
- **India-Bangladesh Border Tensions**: Continued instability in Bangladesh following political transition; India stepped up border fencing and surveillance in West Bengal and Meghalaya to manage cross-border migration.
- **COP30 Preparatory Meeting**: Nations met in Bonn for COP30 preparatory talks; India called for differentiated timelines — developed nations to reach net zero by 2040, developing nations by 2070.

## National
- **Subansiri Lower Hydroelectric Project**: The 2,000 MW **Subansiri Lower Hydroelectric Project** (NHPC) on the Arunachal Pradesh-Assam border received fresh clearances after years of legal delays; power to be shared with northeastern states including AP (through grid).
- **India Semiconductor Mission**: DAC cleared ₹79,000 crore for defence acquisitions involving semiconductor-dependent systems; India Semiconductor Mission separately tracking 4 new fab/ATMP proposals under evaluation.
- **Electoral Rolls — SIR 2025**: ECI's Special Intensive Revision 2025 identified 1.2 crore duplicate/ghost voter entries nationwide; Bihar's voter list revision critical ahead of November elections.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **Amaravati — Integrated Financial City (Oct 31)**: Amaravati was officially recognised as **India's first Integrated Financial City** — a designation that positions it alongside GIFT City (Gujarat) for financial services, fintech, and banking sector development.
- **AP CRDA Projects**: AP Capital Region Development Authority finalised ₹81,317 crore worth of new capital city development projects — roads, utilities, government buildings, green zones, and residential areas in Amaravati.

## Economy & Finance
- **GIFT City Model for Amaravati**: Amaravati's Integrated Financial City status enables tax holidays for financial services firms, single-window clearances, and international financial centre infrastructure — modelled on GIFT City IFSC.

## Science & Technology
- **Subansiri HEP**: 2,000 MW project; on Subansiri river (tributary of Brahmaputra); NHPC project; delayed 15+ years due to environmental and seismic concerns; now back on track.
