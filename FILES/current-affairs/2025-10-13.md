# Current Affairs — Week of October 13, 2025

## International
- **India-Pakistan LoC Ceasefire Review**: India and Pakistan completed an annual review of the 2021 LoC ceasefire agreement; both sides agreed to maintain the understanding amid ongoing diplomatic back-channel talks.
- **India-Japan Semiconductor Partnership**: India and Japan signed a semiconductor partnership MoU — Japan to support India's chip packaging (ATMP) capabilities and share expertise in advanced materials processing.

## National
- **Bihar Election Campaign Begins**: PM Modi, Amit Shah, and Rahul Gandhi kicked off high-voltage campaigns in Bihar; NDA focused on development narrative (Nitish Kumar's infrastructure), INDIA bloc on employment and inflation.
- **Bharat Taxi Announcement**: Government announced **Bharat Taxi** — India's first cooperative cab service — to be launched in Delhi in November 2025, with 650 driver-owners; targets expansion to 20 cities by 2026 and 1 lakh cabs by 2030.
- **POCSO Fast-Track Courts Progress**: National Crime Records Bureau reported 30% reduction in pending POCSO cases in states with dedicated fast-track courts; MHA pushed remaining 12 states to set up FTCs.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP World Bank-ADB Funding for Amaravati**: World Bank and ADB committed **$1.6 billion** (₹13,500 crore) for Amaravati capital city construction — funding roads, water supply, sewage, and public buildings in the core capital area.
- **AP Quantum & AI University**: NIELIT and AP government signed an MoU to establish India's first dedicated **Quantum & Artificial Intelligence University campus in Amaravati** — 133-qubit quantum computer facility planned with IBM, TCS, and L&T as partners.

## Economy & Finance
- **World Bank-ADB Amaravati Funding**: $1.6 billion in multilateral funding secured for AP's capital; conditional on AP's fiscal discipline and timeline adherence; disbursement over 2025-2030.

## Science & Technology
- **133-Qubit Quantum Computer — Amaravati**: AP's planned IBM quantum computer installation (December 2025) will be among India's first commercial-grade quantum computing facilities; supports AI research, drug discovery, and financial modelling.
