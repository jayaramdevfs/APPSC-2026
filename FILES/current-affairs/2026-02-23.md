# Current Affairs — Week of February 23, 2026

## International
- **Vibrant Villages Programme-II (VVP-II)**: Union Home Minister launched VVP-II in Assam's Cachar district — comprehensive development of border villages including road connectivity, solar power, and digital services.
- **India-EU FTA Milestone**: India and EU concluded a key round of FTA negotiations with agreement on intellectual property provisions and sustainable trade standards.

## National
- **NAS Base Year Revised**: MoSPI updated National Accounts Statistics (NAS) base year to **2022-23**, replacing the earlier 2011-12 base. India's GDP growth under new series: **7.6% for 2025-26**.
- **Bonded Labour Rehabilitation Drive**: Government allocated ₹500 crore for rehabilitation of bonded labourers identified in the 50th anniversary survey; AP was among the top states with reported unreleased bonded labourers in agricultural sectors.
- **UPSC Prelims Date Announced**: UPSC announced Civil Services Preliminary Exam 2026 for May 31, triggering significant preparation activity across the country.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Mushroom Mission Announced**: Andhra Pradesh government launched the **Mushroom Mission** with an investment of ₹13,000 crore — targeting 1.62 lakh mushroom production units across AP; aims to make AP India's top mushroom producer.
- **AP Bonded Labour Drive**: AP Labour Department began a district-level survey to identify and rehabilitate bonded agricultural labourers, especially in Kurnool, Anantapur, and Prakasam districts.

## Economy & Finance
- **India Bioeconomy Milestone**: India's bioeconomy reached a record **$195.3 billion** in 2025, contributing nearly 5% to national GDP; Government set a target of $300 billion bioeconomy by 2030.

## Science & Technology
- **Quantum Communication Trials**: DRDO and IIT Delhi completed Phase 1 of quantum key distribution (QKD) network trials over 100 km optical fibre — a step toward tamper-proof government communications.
