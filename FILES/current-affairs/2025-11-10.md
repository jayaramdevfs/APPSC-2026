# Current Affairs — Week of November 10, 2025

## International
- **Ethiopia Hayli Gubbi Volcano**: Ash clouds from the eruption of the **Hayli Gubbi volcano in Ethiopia** (Nov 24-25) caused major disruptions to aviation in Indian airspace — several flights rerouted or delayed, highlighting India's aviation vulnerability to volcanic events.
- **India-UK FTA Update**: India and UK finalised the penultimate chapter of the long-pending FTA covering legal services and intellectual property; deal expected to be signed before March 2026.

## National
- **Bihar Results (Nov 14): NDA Landslide**: BJP-NDA alliance won a decisive majority in Bihar — **BJP became the single largest party in Bihar assembly for the first time**, winning 120+ seats out of 243; INDIA bloc conceded defeat.
- **Red Fort Explosion (Nov 10)**: 15 people killed and 20+ injured in a car explosion near the Red Fort in Delhi on November 10; NIA investigation launched; suspected IED; high security alert in national capital.
- **Nitish Kumar Resignation (Nov 17)**: Bihar CM Nitish Kumar tendered his resignation on November 17 after NDA's Bihar victory, opening the path for BJP to claim the CM post.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Global Investors Summit Preparations**: AP government kicked off preparations for the Grand Global Investors Summit (GIS) 2026 — roadshows planned in Singapore, London, Dubai, and Tokyo to attract $500 billion in investment commitments.
- **AP NIELIT Quantum Campus Progress**: Foundation work for the Quantum & AI University campus in Amaravati advanced; NIELIT signed the land allotment with APCRDA for a 50-acre campus.

## Economy & Finance
- **Bihar Election Economic Signal**: NDA's Bihar win seen as endorsement of welfare + development model; government expected to maintain MGNREGS, PM Awas Yojana, and crop insurance spending into Budget 2026-27.

## Science & Technology
- **Red Fort Explosion Investigation**: NIA and IB teams deployed; preliminary findings suggested remote-triggered IED; security agencies reviewed soft-target protection protocols in all heritage monument areas.
