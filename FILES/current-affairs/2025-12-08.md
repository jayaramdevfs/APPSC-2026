# Current Affairs — Week of December 8, 2025

## International
- **India-New Zealand FTA Concluded**: India and New Zealand concluded FTA negotiations in **9 months** — one of the fastest FTA negotiations for India; aims to **double bilateral trade to USD 5 billion** within 5 years and facilitate USD 20 billion in NZ investments.
- **India-Japan Space Cooperation**: JAXA and ISRO signed a joint lunar exploration MoU — Japan's MMX mission to Martian moons and India's Chandrayaan-4 to have technology sharing components.

## National
- **India Semiconductor Mission — ₹1.6 Lakh Crore Cleared**: India Semiconductor Mission has cleared investment proposals worth ₹1.6 lakh crore as of December 2025; however, absence of a commercial sub-28 nm fabrication plant keeps India dependent on imports for advanced chips.
- **NISAR Launch Preparation**: ISRO and NASA finalized all pre-launch integration for NISAR satellite at Satish Dhawan Space Centre; launch rescheduled to January 2026 after sensor calibration.
- **Subansiri HEP First Power**: Subansiri Lower HEP (2,000 MW) generated its first test power output — 3 of 8 units operational; full commissioning by 2026.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Global Quantum Hub Vision**: AP government announced plans to develop **Amaravati as a Global Quantum Hub** — leveraging the IBM 133-qubit facility, NIELIT campus, and partnerships with startups from IIT Madras and ISB Hyderabad for quantum software development.
- **AP-India-NZ FTA Beneficiaries**: AP's seafood and stone export sectors (Kadapa black galaxy granite, Nellore prawns) identified as major beneficiaries of the India-NZ FTA once it enters force.

## Economy & Finance
- **India's Semiconductor Gap**: Despite clearing ₹1.6 lakh crore investments, India currently lacks advanced logic fabs (sub-28 nm); Micron's Sanand plant handles ATMP only; Tata's fab (in partnership with PSMC) targets 28 nm by 2026-end.

## Science & Technology
- **NISAR Satellite Final Checks**: NISAR (NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar) — dual-frequency SAR (L-band + S-band); will map Earth's surface every 12 days globally; applications in earthquake, glacier, and agricultural change monitoring.
