# Current Affairs — Week of March 30, 2026

## International
- **India-New Zealand FTA Signed (Apr 1 Week)**: Signing ceremony preparations underway in New Delhi ahead of the formal April signing — covers goods (dairy, IT), services, and investment protection.
- **Arctic Ice Coverage Lowest on Record**: Scientists reported Arctic sea ice extent hit an all-time low in March 2026; India's Arctic Policy 2022 gained renewed attention as India's Himadri research station in Svalbard continued monitoring.

## National
- **Madhya Pradesh Bus Accident**: At least 9 people killed and 30 injured when a bus overturned after colliding with a truck in Chhindwara, MP — road safety policies reviewed at the national Road Safety Council.
- **National Road Safety Action Plan**: MoRTH released the updated National Road Safety Action Plan 2026-30, targeting 50% reduction in road deaths by 2030 through better road design, vehicle safety norms, and enforcement.
- **Electoral Bonds Refund Mechanism**: Supreme Court-mandated refund mechanism for expired electoral bonds finalised by ECI and SBI — ₹8,200 crore in bonds to be refunded to donors.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **Polavaram Dam Progress Update**: AP CM Chandrababu Naidu reviewed Polavaram Project progress — earthworks at 95% completion; targeted first impoundment of water by June 2026 monsoon.
- **AP Road Safety Audit**: Following the Markapuram tragedy, AP government initiated a comprehensive safety audit of 500 National Highway black spots across the state; 120 spots identified for immediate repair.

## Economy & Finance
- **Union Budget Implementation — Industrial Clusters**: MoCI approved 45 legacy industrial clusters for revival under Budget 2026-27; AP's Ongole Stainless Steel Cluster and Hindupur Textile Cluster included.

## Science & Technology
- **DRDO Underwater Drone Test**: DRDO successfully tested an autonomous underwater drone (AUV) off the Vishakhapatnam coast — designed for mine detection and underwater surveillance in coastal defence.
