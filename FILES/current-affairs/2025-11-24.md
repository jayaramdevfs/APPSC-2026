# Current Affairs — Week of November 24, 2025

## International
- **Hayli Gubbi Volcano Disruption (Nov 24-25)**: Ash from the Hayli Gubbi volcano (Ethiopia) reached Indian airspace; DGCA issued NOTAMs (Notice to Airmen) rerouting international flights over Indian airspace; airlines absorbed significant fuel cost increases.
- **India COP30 Position Paper**: India submitted its updated climate position paper for COP30 (Brazil, Nov 2025) — reaffirming net zero by 2070, 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030, and demanding $1 trillion climate finance from developed nations.

## National
- **Tenkasi Bus Collision (Nov 24)**: Six people killed and 32 injured in a head-on collision of two private buses in Tenkasi, Tamil Nadu — renewed calls for mandatory speed governors and lane discipline enforcement on state highways.
- **PCPNDT Crackdowns Continue**: MHA data revealed 580+ PCPNDT Act violations detected in October-November; Gujarat, Karnataka, Haryana, and AP among states with highest detection rates.
- **PM Awas Yojana Progress**: MoHUA reported 2.5 crore houses completed under PMAY Gramin (rural) since 2016; Urban Phase 2 awaiting Cabinet approval — to be included in Budget 2026-27.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP PCPNDT Enforcement**: AP ranked 3rd nationally in PCPNDT Act violation detections in Q3 2025; 45 illegal sex determination operators booked across 8 districts; special drive in urban areas of Vijayawada and Guntur.
- **AP Amaravati Infrastructure Milestones**: AP CRDA completed 43 km of internal road network within Amaravati's capital region; utility corridors for water, sewage, and optical fibre laid across 6,800 acres of core capital area.

## Economy & Finance
- **Goldman Sachs India GDP Projection**: Goldman Sachs projected India's real GDP growth at **6.7% for 2026** and **6.8% for 2027** — citing domestic consumption recovery, infrastructure push, and digital economy expansion.

## Science & Technology
- **Global Chess League 2025**: **Alpine SG Pipers** won the **3rd edition of the Global Chess League 2025** title on December 22 — India hosted the event, showcasing India's growing chess ecosystem following Praggnanandhaa's world ranking surge.
