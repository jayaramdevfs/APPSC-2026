# Current Affairs — Week of May 4, 2026

## International
- **India-Oman CEPA to Take Effect (Jun 1)**: India-Oman Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement finalised; expected to be formally notified and come into force June 1, 2026 — AP's marble and granite exporters key beneficiaries.
- **IMF-World Bank Spring Meetings**: India participated actively, pushing for reformed global financial governance, SDR allocation for developing nations, and climate debt swaps.

## National
- **West Bengal CM Sworn In (May 9)**: BJP's Suvendu Adhikari was inaugurated as Chief Minister of West Bengal after the party won a majority in the April elections — ending TMC's 15-year rule.
- **Tamil Nadu CM Vijay Sworn In (May 10)**: Actor-politician Vijay took oath as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu following the TNDMK's historic electoral victory — succeeded DMK's long stint in government.
- **Coal Gasification Scheme Approved (May 13)**: Union Cabinet approved a ₹37,500 crore Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects — aimed at reducing crude oil import dependence.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Google Data Centre Follow-Up**: AP government fast-tracked environmental clearances and infrastructure connectivity (road, power, water) for the Vizag Google AI Hub site; SEZ status proposed.
- **AP Chandrababu's Winning Streak**: AP emerged as top investment destination post-Google announcement; Samsung, Microsoft, and NVIDIA confirmed exploratory talks for AP data centre investments.
- **AP Fisheries & Marble Export**: AP marble (Kadapa black galaxy) and granite industry expected to benefit significantly from India-Oman CEPA, with ₹2,000 crore in additional annual exports projected.

## Economy & Finance
- **Coal/Lignite Gasification**: ₹37,500 crore scheme converts coal to synthetic natural gas (SNG) and liquid fuels — critical for energy security; AP's Singareni coal belt (Telangana, but AP benefits from grid) relevant.
- **India Forex Reserves Update**: India's forex reserves at $722 billion — record high maintained for 3rd consecutive week.

## Science & Technology
- **Ahmedabad-Dholera Semi High-Speed Line Approved (May 13)**: Union Cabinet approved the ₹20,667 crore Ahmedabad–Dholera semi high-speed double line (~134 km) — part of the Dholera Special Investment Region development.
