# Current Affairs — Week of September 15, 2025

## International
- **India-ASEAN Summit**: PM Modi attended the India-ASEAN Summit in Kuala Lumpur; leaders finalised the India-ASEAN Digital Economy Framework and announced the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership review for 2026-30.
- **UN General Assembly (UNGA 80)**: India participated in UNGA's 80th session; External Affairs Minister raised issues of UN Security Council reform, climate finance gap, and Global South representation.

## National
- **UNGA 80 — India's Stand**: India reiterated its demand for permanent UNSC membership; called for equity in climate finance with developed nations meeting the $100 billion annual commitment to developing nations.
- **PM E-DRIVE Scheme Update**: PM E-DRIVE (Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement) disbursed ₹900+ crore since July 2025 for EV two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and e-buses under FAME III successor program.
- **Akash-NG Missile System**: DRDO completed development of Akash-NG (Next Generation) — enhanced anti-drone, cruise missile, and aerial threat interception capability for IAF and Army; cleared for production order.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Digital Bharat Nidhi MoU**: AP government and Digital Bharat Nidhi (Universal Service Obligation Fund) signed an MoU for expedited **Amended BharatNet Programme** rollout — a $16.9 billion mission to bring OFC broadband to every village in AP.
- **AP Clean Energy Target**: AP government announced 30 GW renewable energy target by 2030 — with 12 GW solar already achieved; new wind-solar hybrid parks in Anantapur and Kurnool.

## Economy & Finance
- **PM E-DRIVE Scheme**: Provides demand incentives for electric 2W, 3W, and state government-run e-buses; replaces FAME II; targets 1 crore EV sales by 2027. AP is a major beneficiary with high 3-wheeler adoption in Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam.

## Science & Technology
- **Akash-NG**: Developed by DRDO; uses active electronically scanned array (AESA) radar; range 80 km; can simultaneously track 64 targets; significant upgrade over original Akash system.

## APPSC Angle
> **What to remember for exam:**
> - Akash-NG: DRDO, improved air defence missile; anti-drone + cruise missile interception; AESA radar
> - PM E-DRIVE: EV demand incentive scheme (successor to FAME II); two-wheelers, three-wheelers, buses
> - BharatNet: OFC broadband to villages; Digital Bharat Nidhi (USOF fund) finances it
