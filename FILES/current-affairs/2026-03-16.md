# Current Affairs — Week of March 16, 2026

## International
- **Global Security: Terrorism Trends**: International community saw rise in state-sponsored cyberattacks; India's PRAHAAR strategy gained praise from UN Security Council members as a model national counter-terror framework.
- **India-ASEAN Digital Economy**: India signed the India-ASEAN Digital Economy Framework at ASEAN-India Digital Ministers' Meeting — covering data localisation, cross-border payments, and AI governance.

## National
- **NCERT New Curriculum Framework**: NCERT released updated textbooks under the National Curriculum Framework (NCF-SE 2023) for Classes 6-8, introducing competency-based learning and reducing rote memorisation.
- **Tiger Reserve Buffer Zones**: Supreme Court directed all 54 Tiger Reserves to complete buffer zone notification within 6 months; AP's Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam TR (largest in India) was highlighted.
- **24 Speed Post Expansion**: DoP expanded 24 Speed Post to 12 additional cities including Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada — directly benefiting AP businesses and e-commerce.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve**: Supreme Court directive on buffer zone notifications directly affects NSTR — India's largest tiger reserve (3,568 sq km), spanning AP and Telangana.
- **AP Solar Energy Milestone**: Andhra Pradesh achieved 12 GW of installed solar capacity — among the top 3 solar states in India; new solar parks announced in Kurnool and Anantapur districts.
- **AP Digital Services**: AP's Meeseva 2.0 platform upgraded to AI-assisted grievance resolution; over 400 government services available online through the single-window portal.

## Economy & Finance
- **Industrial Cluster Revival**: Finance Ministry received applications from 17 states for reviving legacy industrial clusters; AP submitted proposals for Ongole Stainless Steel Cluster and Rajam Handloom Cluster.

## Science & Technology
- **Dzukou Valley Wildfire**: IAF continued aerial firefighting missions in the Dzukou Valley (Nagaland-Manipur border) using Mi-17 helicopters with bambi buckets — covering 2,000 hectares affected.
