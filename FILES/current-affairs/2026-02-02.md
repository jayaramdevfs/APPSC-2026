# Current Affairs — Week of February 2, 2026

## International
- **India-US Trade Agreement Progress**: India and the US agreed to fast-track a bilateral trade deal covering IT services, pharmaceuticals, and agriculture; formal negotiations to be completed by Q3 2026.
- **India GDP Growth Revised**: World Bank revised India's GDP growth estimate to 7.5% for 2025-26, citing robust domestic demand and infrastructure spending as key drivers.

## National
- **Union Budget 2026-27 Presented (Feb 1)**: Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman presented the Union Budget:
  - Total expenditure: ₹53,47,315 crore (↑7.7% over 2025-26 RE)
  - Fiscal deficit: 4.3% of GDP (down from 4.4%)
  - **Semiconductor Mission 2.0** launched — ₹76,000 crore package for chip fabrication
  - **Tax holiday until 2047** for foreign companies providing global cloud services via Indian data centres
  - **'High Level Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat'** to be set up
  - 200 legacy industrial clusters to be revived

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Budget Allocations (Union Budget)**: AP received enhanced allocations under PM Awas Yojana, PMGSY, and Jal Jeevan Mission; Special Category Status demand raised again in Parliament.
- **Polavaram Project Funding**: Centre allocated ₹4,200 crore for Polavaram Irrigation Project in the Union Budget — a long-pending AP demand for expediting the national project.

## Economy & Finance
- **Securities Transaction Tax (STT) Increased**: Budget raised STT on futures and options to curb retail speculation and align derivatives taxation with long-term investment incentives.
- **Biopharma SHAKTI Scheme**: 5-year scheme to promote domestic biologics production formally included in budget allocations.

## Science & Technology
- **Semiconductor Mission 2.0**: ₹76,000 crore outlay to set up chip fabrication plants, ATMP (assembly, testing, marking, packaging) facilities, and design-linked incentives for India's semiconductor ecosystem.
