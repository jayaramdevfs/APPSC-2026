# Current Affairs — Week of December 15, 2025

## International
- **India-EU TTC Progress**: India-EU Trade and Technology Council third meeting concluded; agreed on joint AI regulatory framework, mutual recognition of semiconductor standards, and a €500 million India-EU green hydrogen investment fund.
- **WHO Malaria Report**: WHO's World Malaria Report 2025 — India reduced malaria cases by 69% since 2000; AP's malaria burden concentrated in tribal areas of Visakhapatnam and Alluri Sitharama Raju districts.

## National
- **Global Chess League 2025 — Alpine SG Pipers Win (Dec 22)**: **Alpine SG Pipers** clinched the 3rd Global Chess League 2025 title — India's growing chess culture reflected in the league's commercial success and Praggnanandhaa's continued world top-5 ranking.
- **NDMA Disaster Preparedness Report**: NDMA published its annual National Disaster Management Report 2025 — highlighted AP's cyclone early warning system as a national model; recommended expanding coastal shelter network.
- **Akash-NG Operational Trials**: IAF completed operational trials of Akash-NG at Pokhran — system cleared for squadron-level induction; production of 15 batteries to begin Q1 2026.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Cyclone Preparedness Model**: NDMA's 2025 report specifically cited AP's **cyclone early warning and evacuation system** as India's best practice — referencing zero casualties in 3 cyclones since 2023 despite high coastal exposure.
- **AP Malaria Tribal Drive**: AP Health Department launched focused malaria elimination drive in 11 tribal mandals of Alluri Sitharama Raju and Visakhapatnam (Agency areas) — part of National Malaria Elimination Programme 2027.

## Economy & Finance
- **India Forex Reserves**: India's forex reserves at ₹685 billion — providing 11 months of import cover; RBI maintained a build-up policy amid global currency volatility.

## Science & Technology
- **Magnetic Nanobot Cancer Therapy**: IISc team's breakthrough entered Phase 1 clinical trials at AIIMS New Delhi — using externally controlled magnetic fields to steer drug-loaded nanobots to tumour sites in breast and prostate cancer patients.
