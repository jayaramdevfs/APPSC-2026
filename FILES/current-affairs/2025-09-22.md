# Current Affairs — Week of September 22, 2025

## International
- **India-Canada Diplomatic Tension**: India-Canada relations remained strained following unresolved issues around the Nijjar case; India recalled diplomats and imposed visa restrictions; bilateral trade discussions paused.
- **Global Climate Week (New York)**: India presented its updated National Determined Contribution (NDC) at Climate Week — targeting 500 GW non-fossil fuel capacity by 2030 and net zero by 2070.

## National
- **PCPNDT Act Crackdown**: Fresh crackdowns in Karnataka, AP, Haryana, Gujarat, and Delhi exposed cross-border sex-determination rackets; despite 30+ years of the PCPNDT Act (1994), illegal pre-natal sex determination persists in rural areas.
- **Jan Aushadhi Kendras — 17,500+ Centers**: PM Jan Aushadhi Pariyojana reached 17,500+ stores nationwide offering ~2,110 generic medicines and 315 surgical items at 50-90% less than market price.
- **National Logistics Policy Review**: MoCI reviewed implementation of the National Logistics Policy (2022); India's logistics cost reduced from 14% to 11.8% of GDP — target is 8% by 2030.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **PCPNDT Act — AP Action**: AP government conducted statewide raids under the PCPNDT Act; over 40 illegal ultrasound operators booked in Guntur, Krishna, and Kurnool districts; FIRs registered.
- **AP Jan Aushadhi Expansion**: AP added 280 new Jan Aushadhi Kendras in PHCs and CHCs in tribal and rural areas; AP now has 2,100+ Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Kendras — among top states.

## Economy & Finance
- **India Logistics Cost**: Reduction in logistics cost to 11.8% of GDP — critical for export competitiveness; AP's Krishnapatnam and Kamarajar ports benefiting from multimodal logistics hub development.

## Science & Technology
- **Alaknanda Galaxy Discovered**: Indian astronomers (IUCAA team) discovered **Alaknanda** — an unexpectedly well-formed spiral galaxy dating to just 1.5 billion years after the Big Bang; challenges standard galaxy formation models.
