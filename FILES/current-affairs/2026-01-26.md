# Current Affairs — Week of January 26, 2026

## International
- **India Republic Day — Chief Guest**: Republic Day 2026 celebrated with a foreign head of state as Chief Guest; theme focused on Viksit Bharat 2047 and India's growing strategic stature.
- **Global AI Governance Framework**: G20 nations discussed binding AI governance norms; India advocated for a multi-stakeholder, development-friendly framework rejecting blanket restrictions.

## National
- **Republic Day Parade (Jan 26)**: India's 77th Republic Day featured AP's tableau, live rehearsal of indigenous defence systems including Pralay, BrahMos, and Sudarshan Chakra.
- **Immunity for Election Commissioners Struck Down**: Parliament debated amendments after a review committee flagged that the Chief Election Commissioner & Other Election Commissioners Act, 2023 needed stronger independence protections.
- **Biopharma SHAKTI Scheme (announced ahead of Budget)**: Centre announced 5-year Biopharma SHAKTI scheme to enable domestic production of biologics and biopharmaceuticals, boosting India's pharma sector.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Tableau at Republic Day**: Andhra Pradesh's tableau at the Republic Day parade showcased Amaravati's development model and the state's cultural heritage.
- **AP Police Modernisation**: AP government sanctioned ₹800 crore for police infrastructure modernisation — new vehicles, body cameras, and digital FIR systems for all police stations.

## Economy & Finance
- **Pre-Budget Economic Survey**: Ministry of Finance tabled the Economic Survey 2025-26 ahead of the Union Budget, projecting 7.4–7.8% GDP growth for FY2026-27 backed by strong domestic consumption.

## Science & Technology
- **ISRO PSLV-C63 Launch**: ISRO's PSLV-C63 successfully placed the NVS-02 navigation satellite into orbit, strengthening the NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) system.

> **What to remember for exam:**
> - Republic Day: January 26 each year; India became Republic on Jan 26, 1950
> - NavIC: India's indigenous navigation satellite system (alternative to GPS) — fully operational with NVS series
> - Biopharma SHAKTI: 5-year scheme for domestic biologics production
