# Current Affairs — Week of February 9, 2026

## International
- **India-Israel Special Strategic Partnership**: PM Modi visited Israel; both nations elevated bilateral ties to a "Special Strategic Partnership" and signed 17 pacts covering defence technology, cybersecurity, water management, and agricultural innovation.
- **Pax Silica Initiative**: India launched the Pax Silica Initiative — a strategic move to secure the silicon (semiconductor) supply chain by partnering with mineral-rich nations and investing in domestic refining capacity.

## National
- **16th Finance Commission Report**: The 16th Finance Commission (Chair: Dr. Arvind Panagariya) submitted its report:
  - States' share of central taxes maintained at **41%**
  - Centre recommended to reduce fiscal deficit to **3.5% of GDP by 2030-31**
  - Strengthened grants for health and local body governance
- **RBI Monetary Policy (Feb 6)**: MPC voted to hold repo rate at **5.25%** (neutral stance); RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra cited external headwinds and ongoing trade deals as reasons for pause.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Water Management Innovation**: AP signed an MoU with Israel for drip irrigation technology, micro-irrigation for horticulture, and wastewater recycling — part of the India-Israel strategic partnership outcomes.
- **AP Fisheries Boost**: AP government launched a ₹1,200 crore scheme to develop deep-sea fishing infrastructure at Kakinada and Visakhapatnam ports, targeting doubling fisheries exports by 2028.

## Economy & Finance
- **RBI Repo Rate Held at 5.25%**: MPC decision — keeping borrowing costs stable while monitoring food inflation and global trade conditions before next cut.
- **16th Finance Commission**: States to get 41% of divisible pool; AP expected enhanced grants under the devolution formula.

## Science & Technology
- **India-AI Impact Summit 2026**: India asserted its right to develop a "sovereign vertical technology stack" — indigenous AI models, cloud, and data infrastructure — rejecting full dependence on foreign platforms.
