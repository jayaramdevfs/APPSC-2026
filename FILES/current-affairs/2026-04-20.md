# Current Affairs — Week of April 20, 2026

## International
- **West Bengal Elections (Apr 23-29)**: West Bengal Legislative Assembly elections took place across 5 phases; BJP and AITMC in key contest; ECI deployed maximum central forces in history for a single state election.
- **India-EU Digital Trade Pact**: India and EU signed a Digital Trade chapter MoU — covering data flows, AI governance, and cybersecurity standards as part of the broader Trade and Technology Council (TTC) framework.

## National
- **Model Code of Conduct — West Bengal**: ECI enforced strict MCC with multiple violations notices to political parties; 500+ seizures of cash and liquor worth ₹450 crore.
- **NCERT Textbook Controversy**: New NCERT Class 12 Political Science textbooks drew controversy for revised content on Emergency period and certain historical events; Parliament debated editorial independence.
- **PM Awas Yojana 2.0 Targets**: Government announced Phase 2 targets — 2 crore more urban houses by 2029 under PMAY Urban 2.0 with enhanced subsidies for EWS/LIG categories.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Industrialisation Push**: AP CM Chandrababu Naidu attended Global Investors Summit preparatory roadshow in Singapore — pitching AP's investment climate, Amaravati development, and coastal economic zone opportunities.
- **AP Jagananna Amma Vodi Update**: AP government continued the Jagananna Amma Vodi scheme — ₹15,000 annual support to poor mothers sending children to school; over 43 lakh beneficiaries in 2025-26.
- **Vizag Smart City Projects**: Visakhapatnam Smart City projects worth ₹2,200 crore entered final execution phase — smart roads, integrated traffic management, and coastal promenade development.

## Economy & Finance
- **RBI Annual Report Preview**: RBI published its half-year macro stability report — currency account deficit stable at 1.2% of GDP, credit growth at 14.3%, and gross NPA ratio at 3-year low of 2.8%.

## Science & Technology
- **Gaganyaan Update**: ISRO announced the unmanned Gaganyaan mission (TV-D2) successfully completed abort test — paving way for the crewed mission scheduled for late 2026 or early 2027.
