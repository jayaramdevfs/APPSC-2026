# Current Affairs — Week of October 27, 2025

## International
- **India-France Strategic Partnership**: India and France signed 12 bilateral agreements covering nuclear energy (Jaitapur update), defence (Rafale Marine jets for Indian Navy), and space cooperation (ISRO-CNES joint missions).
- **Global Hunger Index 2025**: India ranked 105 out of 127 countries in the Global Hunger Index 2025 — government rejected the methodology; nutrition indicators improving under PM Poshan, Anaemia Mukt Bharat, and ICDS.

## National
- **Bihar Pre-Election Final Week**: NDA and INDIA bloc final rallies; NDA led in surveys by 15-20 seats. ECI seized ₹280 crore cash and liquor across Bihar in the pre-poll week — record seizure.
- **India-NZ FTA Final Round**: India and New Zealand completed final-round FTA negotiations; agreement expected to be signed in Q1 2026; dairy remains a key sticking point with India seeking protection for domestic milk farmers.
- **Bharat Taxi Launch Preparations**: Delhi government and ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) finalised the Bharat Taxi operational framework ahead of November launch.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Foundation for Amaravati PSBs**: CM Chandrababu Naidu and Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman laid the **foundation stone for regional headquarters of 15 Public Sector Banks and Insurance Companies** in Amaravati — a landmark for the capital's financial district.
- **AP Fisheries Milestone**: AP achieved 30 lakh metric tonnes of fish production in 2025 — highest ever; prawn exports from Godavari-Krishna belt contributed ₹18,000 crore in foreign exchange.

## Economy & Finance
- **ONDC Expansion**: ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) expanded to 236 cities; transaction volumes crossed 10 lakh per day; Bharat Taxi, Bharat Rides among new verticals being onboarded.

## Science & Technology
- **Jaitapur Nuclear Power Project**: India-France progress on Jaitapur NPP (6 × 1,650 MW EPR reactors in Maharashtra) — final negotiations on cost and technology transfer terms; largest planned nuclear plant in the world.

> **What to remember for exam:**
> - Amaravati PSB HQs: Foundation stone by CM Naidu + FM Sitharaman; 15 PSBs and insurance companies
> - ONDC: Open Network for Digital Commerce; government-backed; democratises digital commerce
> - Jaitapur NPP: Maharashtra; 6 EPR reactors; India-France project; total 9,900 MW when complete
