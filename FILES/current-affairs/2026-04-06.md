# Current Affairs — Week of April 6, 2026

## International
- **India-New Zealand FTA Signed (Apr 28)**: Formal signing of the New Zealand–India Free Trade Agreement in New Delhi — India's latest trade partnership covering goods, services, and investment.
- **G20 Sherpa Meeting**: India, as G20 troika member, participated in sherpa-level meetings focusing on digital economy governance and debt restructuring for Global South nations.

## National
- **Amaravati Declared Sole Capital of AP (Apr 7)**: The **Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026** received President's assent on April 7 and was published in the Gazette — officially declaring Amaravati as the sole capital of Andhra Pradesh, ending years of the three-capital controversy.
- **AP Reorganisation Amendment Bill**: The Bill was introduced in Lok Sabha on April 1 and passed by both Houses; CM Chandrababu Naidu thanked PM Modi and President Murmu.
- **West Bengal Assembly Elections Announced**: ECI announced West Bengal Legislative Assembly elections from April 23-29, with counting on May 2.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **Amaravati Sole Capital — Historic Moment**: AP's prolonged capital crisis resolved — Amaravati declared the single capital, reversing the three-capital (Amaravati + Kurnool + Visakhapatnam) policy of the previous YSRCP government.
  - **Significance**: Provides clarity for investors; unlocks ₹58,000 crore capital development projects
  - **CM Naidu** stated: "This is a historic day for AP's future and Amaravati's global ambitions"
- **AP Capital Development**: Capital construction works worth ₹56,000 crore underway; completion target 2028 for first phase of Secretariat, High Court, and Legislative Assembly buildings.

## Economy & Finance
- **Jagan Terms Amaravati 'Capital of Corruption'**: Opposition YSRCP leader Jagan Mohan Reddy criticised the Amaravati decision and announced a statewide agitation — highlighting continued political polarisation in AP.

## Science & Technology
- **India AI Hub Foundation**: India formally broke ground for a national AI Hub cluster with data centre infrastructure, GPU clouds, and AI startup incubation, coordinated with Google's upcoming investment.
