# Current Affairs — Week of March 9, 2026

## International
- **India Wins T20 World Cup 2026**: India beat New Zealand in the final on **March 8** to win the Men's T20 World Cup 2026 — India's third T20 World Cup title. The tournament was hosted across multiple venues in South Africa.
- **India Hosts 2028 World Athletics Indoor Championships**: Bhubaneswar (Odisha) was selected as the host city for the **2028 World Athletics Indoor Championships** — making India the fourth Asian nation to host the event.

## National
- **Quantum National Mission Update**: Phase 2 of the National Quantum Mission launched — ₹3,000 crore for quantum computers, quantum communication networks, and quantum sensing applications across 4 national quantum hubs.
- **India-Oman CEPA Negotiations**: India and Oman finalised most chapters of the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA), expected to come into force by June 2026.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Cricket Infrastructure**: Vizag's Dr. Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy ACA-VDCA Stadium was selected as one of India's venues for upcoming international cricket fixtures — boosting sports tourism in AP.
- **AP Horticulture Mission**: AP government launched a ₹2,800 crore horticulture mission targeting mango, banana, chilli, and turmeric value chains; AP is India's largest producer of chillies.

## Economy & Finance
- **India-Oman CEPA**: Trade deal covers goods, services, and investment — India expects $5 billion in incremental exports to Oman and the GCC region once in force.
- **Union Budget Implementation**: Finance Ministry issued detailed implementation guidelines for the Semiconductor Mission 2.0 and industrial cluster revival scheme; states invited to nominate clusters by April.

## Science & Technology
- **DRDO Hypersonic Missile Test**: DRDO successfully tested a hypersonic technology demonstrator vehicle (HSTDV) at speeds above Mach 7 — advancing India's hypersonic glide vehicle program.
