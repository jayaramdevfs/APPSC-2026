# Current Affairs — Week of January 19, 2026

## International
- **Arctic Thawing Accelerates**: New studies confirmed Arctic is warming four times faster than the global average; permafrost thaw releasing methane threatening to accelerate climate change feedback loops.
- **India-US Tech Partnership**: India and US signed MOUs on semiconductor supply chains and critical minerals cooperation under the iCET (Initiative on Critical and Emerging Technologies) framework.

## National
- **16th Finance Commission (Interim)**: Finance Commission chaired by Dr. Arvind Panagariya released interim recommendations ahead of full report; focused on vertical and horizontal devolution to states.
- **Anti-Narcotics Reforms (NCORD)**: Cabinet approved reforms to the Narcotics Control Coordination Centre (NCORD), enhancing inter-agency intelligence sharing and creating fast-track courts for drug trafficking cases.
- **K-4 Submarine Launched Ballistic Missile**: DRDO successfully test-fired the K-4 SLBM (range ~3,500 km) from an underwater platform off the AP coast, strengthening India's second-strike nuclear capability.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **K-4 SLBM Test off AP Coast**: DRDO conducted the K-4 SLBM test launch from the Bay of Bengal near the AP coast — significant for India's nuclear triad completeness.
- **AP Tourism Boost**: State government announced ₹2,500 crore investment in coastal tourism circuits — Vizag, Araku, Bheemunipatnam — targeting 10 crore tourists by 2028.

## Economy & Finance
- **NAS New Base Year**: MoSPI revised National Accounts Statistics (NAS) base year from 2011-12 to 2022-23, providing updated GDP/GVA measurement framework. GDP growth estimated at 7.6% for 2025-26.

## Science & Technology
- **Quantum Military Integration**: Indian Army and DRDO began Phase 1 of quantum key distribution (QKD) trials for secure military communications; part of India's National Quantum Mission.

> - K-4 SLBM: range 3,500 km, nuclear-capable, launched from submarine — part of India's nuclear triad
