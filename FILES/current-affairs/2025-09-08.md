# Current Affairs — Week of September 8, 2025

## International
- **India Wins Hockey Asia Cup 2025**: India defeated Korea **4-1 in the final** at Rajgir, Bihar — India's **4th Men's Hockey Asia Cup** title; team sealed direct qualification for the **2026 FIH Men's Hockey World Cup** (Belgium/Netherlands).
- **India-EU Critical Minerals MoU**: India and EU finalised a critical minerals partnership covering lithium, cobalt, and rare earths — crucial for India's EV battery and semiconductor supply chains.

## National
- **C.P. Radhakrishnan Elected 15th Vice President**: CP Radhakrishnan was elected India's **15th Vice President** on September 9, 2025, defeating Justice B. Sudershan Reddy with 452 votes and over 98% MP turnout.
  - Born: October 20, 1957, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu
  - Previously served as Governor of Maharashtra, Jharkhand; held additional charge of Telangana & Puducherry
- **SOAR Curriculum Rollout**: SOAR (Skilling for AI Readiness) AI curriculum piloted in 6 states; structured modules for students and teacher training in AI ethics and applications.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **CP Radhakrishnan — AP Connection**: As former Governor of Telangana (additional charge), CP Radhakrishnan is familiar with AP-Telangana bifurcation issues; his election as VP seen positively for AP's pending special status discussions.
- **AP Hockey Development**: AP sent 5 players to the national Hockey Asia Cup squad; state government announced ₹150 crore for Astroturf upgrades and hockey academies in Kurnool and East Godavari.

## Economy & Finance
- **India Semiconductor Mission Progress**: India Semiconductor Mission cleared ₹1.6 lakh crore in investment proposals; Micron's ATMP plant in Sanand, Gujarat entered trial production ahead of schedule.

## Science & Technology
- **India Hockey Infrastructure**: Rajgir (Bihar) hosted the Hockey Asia Cup — India's first major global hockey tournament in a Tier-2 city, showcasing sports infrastructure development under Khelo India.
