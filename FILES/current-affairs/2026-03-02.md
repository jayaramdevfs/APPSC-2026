# Current Affairs — Week of March 2, 2026

## International
- **India Bioeconomy Leadership**: India formally presented its bioeconomy roadmap at the Global Bio Innovation Summit — $195 billion economy, targeting $300 billion by 2030 via biopharma, biofuels, bioplastics, and agri-biotech.
- **CBAM Impact Assessment**: India's Ministry of Commerce released a report on the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism's impact — estimated ₹12,000 crore annual impact on Indian steel/cement exports by 2026-27.

## National
- **Bihar Political Crisis**: Bihar CM Nitish Kumar announced his resignation to contest Rajya Sabha elections (March 4); precipitating a political realignment in the state.
- **Legacy Thrust Territories**: Ministry of Home Affairs introduced "Legacy Thrust Territories" — districts formerly affected by Left Wing Extremism (Naxalism) where security presence continues even after operational gains, to prevent resurgence.
- **24 Speed Post Launched**: Department of Posts launched **24 Speed Post** — next-day guaranteed delivery service initially in 6 metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru), later to expand nationwide.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Naxal-Free Certification**: Several former Left Wing Extremism (LWE)-affected districts in AP (Alluri Sitharama Raju, Visakhapatnam tribal areas) were declared LWE-free under the Legacy Thrust Territories framework.
- **AP Agriculture Recovery**: Post-cyclone agricultural recovery program in AP's coastal Krishna-Godavari delta intensified with seed banks and crop insurance disbursals to 8 lakh affected farmers.

## Economy & Finance
- **Indian Bioeconomy Policy**: BIRAC (Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council) released a 5-year bioeconomy strategy aligning with the Biopharma SHAKTI scheme and AtmaNirbhar Bharat vision.

## Science & Technology
- **ISRO PSLV-C64**: ISRO announced the PSLV-C64 mission to place a constellation of earth observation satellites — aimed at real-time disaster monitoring and agricultural crop assessment.

## APPSC Angle
> **What to remember for exam:**

> - 24 Speed Post: DoP's next-day guaranteed delivery (6 metro cities initially)
> - BIRAC: Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council — under DBT (Dept. of Biotechnology)
