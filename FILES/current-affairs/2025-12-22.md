# Current Affairs — Week of December 22, 2025

## International
- **India-Israel Strategic Partnership Outcomes**: Following the November PM Modi visit, India and Israel fast-tracked implementation of 17 bilateral pacts — first shipment of Israeli Heron TP MALE drones arrived for IAF evaluation; water management tech transfer to AP and Rajasthan.
- **Goldman Sachs India GDP 2026**: Goldman Sachs forecast India's real GDP growth at **6.7% for 2026** and **6.8% for 2027** — making India the fastest-growing major economy for the 5th consecutive year.

## National
- **ISRO Heaviest Launch (Dec 24)**: ISRO launched its **heaviest payload** — a US-built **AST SpaceMobile communications satellite** weighing **6,100 kg** — using LVM3 (GSLV Mk III); demonstrates India's launch vehicle capacity for commercial heavy payloads.
- **Rashtra Prerna Sthal Inaugurated (Dec 25)**: PM Modi inaugurated the **Rashtra Prerna Sthal** in Lucknow on December 25 — the 101st birth anniversary of former PM Atal Bihari Vajpayee; a cultural-motivational centre celebrating India's freedom fighters and visionary leaders.
- **AP Quantum Hub Plan Announced (Dec 23)**: AP formally announced plans to build a **Global Quantum Hub in Amaravati** — with IBM quantum computing, NIELIT campus, and startup incubation for quantum software and applications.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Quantum Hub Announcement**: AP positioned Amaravati as India's first Quantum Valley — **133-qubit IBM quantum computer** under installation; NIELIT's quantum curriculum to go live in 2026; partnerships with NASSCOM and IIT Hyderabad for quantum software bootcamps.
- **AP Year-End Investment Review**: AP's Year-End review 2025 showed the state secured ₹10 lakh crore ($120 billion) in MoUs — top sectors: data centres, semiconductors, green energy, chemicals, and food processing.

## Economy & Finance
- **AST SpaceMobile Commercial Revenue**: ISRO earned commercial launch revenue of $195 million from the AST SpaceMobile mission — part of ISRO's growing commercial launch business via NewSpace India Ltd (NSIL).

## Science & Technology
- **AP Quantum Valley**: Amaravati Quantum Hub covers: IBM 133-qubit quantum computer (installations), NIELIT quantum curriculum, quantum-safe cybersecurity research, and drug discovery using quantum simulation — AP's bid to lead India's quantum economy.
