# Current Affairs — Week of January 5, 2026

## International
- **India-EU FTA Talks**: India and the EU resumed negotiations on a Free Trade Agreement aimed at removing tariffs on 90%+ of traded goods, boosting Indian exports in textiles and leather while improving mobility for Indian professionals.
- **AI Carbon Footprint Debate**: Global concern grew over AI's energy consumption; India committed to Responsible AI under the IndiaAI Mission with safeguards for sustainability.

## National
- **Global AI Summit 2026**: India hosted the Global AI Summit 2026, positioning itself as a major player in responsible AI governance and digital infrastructure.
- **Param Shakti Supercomputer**: Installed at IIT Madras under the National Supercomputing Mission — delivers 3.1 petaflop performance, boosting India's HPC capacity.
- **Deep Tech Startup Policy**: Deep Tech startups redefined — now allowed 20 years and ₹300 crore turnover limit to qualify for startup benefits under DPIIT.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Smart Ration Cards**: Andhra Pradesh continued distribution of Smart Ration Cards to all ration card holders, integrating Aadhaar biometrics for leakage-free PDS delivery.
- **AP Infrastructure Push**: State government accelerated infrastructure project clearances ahead of the Amaravati capital construction timeline.

## Economy & Finance
- **RBI Monetary Policy Shift**: RBI began the year with a signal of shifting toward neutral stance as inflation eased; analysts expected rate cuts in Q1 2026 if food prices stabilised.

## Science & Technology
- **IndiaAI Mission Expansion**: Government expanded the IndiaAI Mission with new compute capacity, AI skilling programs, and regulatory frameworks targeting 5 lakh AI-trained professionals by 2027.

## APPSC Angle
> **What to remember for exam:**
> - Param Shakti Supercomputer — IIT Madras, 3.1 petaflops, National Supercomputing Mission
