# Current Affairs — Week of October 6, 2025

## International
- **Nobel Prize Announcements**: Nobel Prize week 2025 — Nobel Peace Prize, Economics, Literature, Physics, Chemistry, and Medicine announcements dominated global news; India's research contributions acknowledged in Physics and Medicine citations.
- **India-EU TTC Meeting**: India-EU Trade and Technology Council (TTC) meeting held in Brussels; agreed on semiconductor cooperation, AI standards alignment, and joint climate tech investment fund.

## National
- **Bihar Assembly Elections Announced**: Election Commission of India announced Bihar Legislative Assembly elections in 2 phases — **Phase 1: November 6** and **Phase 2: November 11, 2025**; counting on November 14.
- **Special Intensive Revision (SIR) 2025**: ECI launched the **Special Intensive Revision (SIR) 2025** — a document-based large-scale voter roll verification exercise across all states; first comprehensive revision in nearly two decades.
- **Mine-Water Innovation — Jharkhand**: ACIC IIT-ISM Dhanbad's Water ATM initiative for mine-water reuse in Jharkhand's coal belts — transforms coal mine discharge into clean drinking water for local communities.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Tourism Global Award (Oct 4)**: AP Tourism Department won the **Global Tourism Award 2025** in New Delhi — recognising AP's integrated coastal tourism development, eco-tourism initiatives in Araku Valley, and Tirupati heritage tourism.
- **APCRDA Office Inauguration**: CM Chandrababu Naidu inaugurated the AP Capital Region Development Authority (APCRDA) office in Amaravati — a 7-storey, 28,562 sq m building; serves as the nerve centre for capital city planning.

## Economy & Finance
- **DAC Approvals — ₹79,000 Crore**: Defence Acquisition Council (DAC) accorded Acceptance of Necessity (AoN) for capital acquisition proposals worth ₹79,000 crore — covering fighter jet upgrades, naval vessels, and next-gen artillery systems.

## Science & Technology
- **ICGS Ajit & Aparajit Launched**: Indian Coast Guard's two new **Fast Patrol Vessels (FPVs) — ICGS Ajit and ICGS Aparajit** — launched at Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL) under Make in India; part of 8 indigenously built FPVs order.
