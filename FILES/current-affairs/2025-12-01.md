# Current Affairs — Week of December 1, 2025

## International
- **COP30 Outcomes (Belém, Brazil)**: COP30 concluded with the **Belém Declaration** — developed nations committed to $300 billion annual climate finance by 2035; India secured recognition of "differentiated responsibilities" principle for net zero timelines.
- **India-UK FTA Signed**: India and the United Kingdom formally signed the **India-UK Free Trade Agreement** — covers 99% of tariff lines, reduced duties on Indian textiles, pharmaceuticals, and IT services; UK gains market access for whisky and automobiles.

## National
- **AP National Energy Conservation Award (Dec 2)**: Andhra Pradesh received the **National Energy Conservation Award** for the **4th consecutive year** on December 2 — recognising AP's achievement in renewable energy adoption, energy efficiency in industry, and smart grid implementation.
- **DAC ₹79,000 Crore Approvals**: Defence Acquisition Council finalised ₹79,000 crore in Acceptance of Necessity (AoN) for capital equipment — including next-gen fighter jet upgrades, P-8I follow-on order, and Pinaka multi-barrel rocket launchers.
- **India-UK FTA Coverage**: UK is India's 6th largest trading partner; deal expected to add $15-20 billion to bilateral trade by 2030; AP's textile and pharma exporters are key beneficiaries.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **National Energy Conservation Award (4th Consecutive)**: AP's energy department awarded for excellence in renewable integration, MSME energy audits, and the state's smart metering rollout under RDSS (Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme).
- **AP RDSS Progress**: AP's Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme targets 100% smart prepaid meters for all consumers by March 2026; reduces AT&C losses from 18% to 12%.

## Economy & Finance
- **India-UK FTA Economic Impact**: FTA expected to boost India's services exports (especially IT and financial services) and gives Indian professionals easier work visa access to the UK; AP's IT corridor (Hyderabad-adjacent) indirectly benefits.

## Science & Technology
- **Akash-NG Production Order**: Following development completion, MoD placed an initial production order for 15 Akash-NG missile batteries — to equip IAF squadrons by 2027; replaces imported short-range SAMs.
