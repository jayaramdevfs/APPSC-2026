# Current Affairs — Week of April 27, 2026

## International
- **New Zealand–India FTA Signed (Apr 28)**: India and New Zealand formally signed the Free Trade Agreement in New Delhi — India's 14th FTA; covers dairy (limited access), IT services, education, and investment.
- **Google Breaks Ground on India AI Hub (Apr 28)**: Google launched construction of its AI data centre campus near Visakhapatnam (AP) as part of its $1.35 lakh crore India investment — designated "Asia's largest AI data centre facility."

## National
- **West Bengal Results (Counting: May 2)**: Exit polls predicted BJP majority in West Bengal; Suvendu Adhikari emerged as likely CM candidate.
- **IMF World Economic Outlook (April 2026)**: IMF revised India's GDP growth forecast to **6.5% for FY 2026-27**, citing strong domestic consumption but external headwinds from global trade slowdowns.
- **India-US Bilateral Trade Agreement**: India and US issued a joint statement confirming the framework for a bilateral trade agreement covering pharmaceuticals, IT, and agricultural market access.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **Google AI Data Centre — Visakhapatnam**: CM Chandrababu Naidu laid the foundation for Google's AI data centre near Vizag (Apr 28) — ₹1.35 lakh crore investment; creates 15,000 direct and 60,000 indirect jobs; described as "pride of North Andhra."
- **5th Gen Fighter Jet Factory — Puttaparthi**: AP Cabinet approved 600 acres in Puttaparthi for DRDO's Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA) — India's indigenous 5th generation stealth fighter manufacturing facility.
- **IMF Growth for AP**: AP's GSDP growth target for 2026-27 revised to 9.2% — above national average, driven by capital city construction, IT investment, and industrial growth.

## Economy & Finance
- **IMF Forecast**: India at 6.5% growth (FY2026-27); global growth revised to 3.1%; India remains fastest-growing major economy for 4th consecutive year.

## Science & Technology
- **AMCA Project**: Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (5th gen stealth fighter) — DRDO-led program; Puttaparthi (AP) selected for manufacturing; reduces dependence on foreign fighter jet imports.
