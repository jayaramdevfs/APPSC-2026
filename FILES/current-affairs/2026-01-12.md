# Current Affairs — Week of January 12, 2026

## International
- **Himalayan Ecocide Warning**: Scientists flagged accelerating glacial retreat across the Hindu Kush-Himalayan region, threatening water security for over a billion people; India called for emergency adaptation funding at a UN climate meet.
- **EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)**: EU's CBAM fully kicked in, affecting Indian steel and aluminium exports; India began lobbying for exemptions and reviewing carbon pricing strategies.

## National
- **National Youth Day (Jan 12)**: Commemorated Swami Vivekananda's birth anniversary; PM addressed youth at a national conclave emphasising skill development under Viksit Bharat 2047.
- **Sudarshan Chakra Air Defence**: Indian Army operationalised the Sudarshan Chakra short-range air defence system, capable of neutralising drones and low-altitude threats.
- **POCSO/RTE Reforms**: Parliament debated amendments to POCSO Act for faster trials and revisions to RTE to include private unaided schools in financial support frameworks.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Farmer Welfare**: AP government released the latest instalment of direct benefit transfers under farmer welfare schemes, covering over 40 lakh beneficiaries across the state.
- **AP Skill Development**: State government announced expansion of AP Skill Development Corporation training centres in 5 new districts to boost youth employability.

## Economy & Finance
- **Groundwater Crisis Report**: MoJS (Ministry of Jal Shakti) published a report highlighting severe groundwater depletion in 8 states including Andhra Pradesh; new regulation for industrial groundwater extraction proposed.

## Science & Technology
- **Pralay Missile Operationalised**: Indian Army formally inducted the Pralay surface-to-surface tactical missile (range 150–500 km) into frontline units, boosting conventional deterrence.
