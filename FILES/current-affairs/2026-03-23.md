# Current Affairs — Week of March 23, 2026

## International
- **India-New Zealand FTA Finalised**: India and New Zealand finalised the framework for a Free Trade Agreement covering dairy (sensitive sector for India), IT services, and wool/textiles — formal signing expected in April.
- **Climate Finance Gap**: UN Climate Report flagged a $4 trillion annual climate finance gap for developing nations; India called for mandatory contributions from developed countries at a G20 climate finance meeting.

## National
- **NIA Sentences Asiya Andrabi**: A Special NIA court sentenced Kashmiri separatist Asiya Andrabi to **life imprisonment** (March 24) for waging war against India and providing material support to terrorist organisations.
- **POCSO Fast-Track Courts**: Parliament passed amendments directing states to set up dedicated POCSO fast-track courts in every district with 100+ pending cases; AP to set up 23 new FTCs.
- **Legacy Thrust Territories Implementation**: MHA published the final list of 90 districts across 8 states under Legacy Thrust Territories for continued security and development attention.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Bus Accident — Markapuram (Mar 25)**: A tragic bus-lorry collision in Markapuram (Prakasam district) killed 14 people and injured 20; AP government announced compensation and initiated road safety audit on NH-516.
- **AP POCSO Fast-Track Courts**: AP government announced 23 new fast-track courts specifically for POCSO cases — aligning with the national mandate, funded through Nirbhaya Fund.

## Economy & Finance
- **India Forex Reserves**: India's foreign exchange reserves reached $720 billion — a record high, providing strong import cover of ~10 months and currency stability buffer.

## Science & Technology
- **India's Bioeconomy Policy Paper**: NITI Aayog released a policy paper on Blue Economy — targeting $1 trillion ocean economy by 2030; AP's coastline (972 km) identified as a key growth corridor.
