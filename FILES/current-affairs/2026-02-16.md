# Current Affairs — Week of February 16, 2026

## International
- **RIN Revolt 80th Anniversary**: India commemorated the 80th anniversary of the Royal Indian Navy Revolt (February 18, 1946) — when naval ratings in Bombay rose against British rule, marking the beginning of the end of colonial rule.
- **Forest Fires in Northeast India**: Massive fires in Arunachal Pradesh (Lohit Valley) and Nagaland (Dzukou Valley) forced the Indian Air Force to conduct high-altitude aerial firefighting operations — raising concerns about climate-driven disaster frequency.

## National
- **PRAHAAR — India's Counter-Terror Strategy**: Ministry of Home Affairs released **PRAHAAR**, India's first publicly articulated national counter-terrorism strategy document. It outlines multi-agency coordination, intelligence fusion, and zero-tolerance for terror financing.
- **50th Anniversary — Bonded Labour Abolition Act**: Nation marked 50 years of the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976; government launched a rehabilitation survey identifying ~15,000 unreported cases.
- **New Ramsar Sites Added**: India added 4 new wetlands to the Ramsar Convention list, taking India's total to 89 — highest in Asia. AP's Kolleru Lake remains one of India's largest freshwater Ramsar sites.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **Kolleru Wetland Conservation**: AP government launched a ₹320 crore action plan for Kolleru Lake restoration — reducing encroachments, restoring bird habitats, and regulating aquaculture.
- **AP IAF Base Modernisation**: Vizag Air Force Station received new infrastructure investments as part of India's northeast and coastal defence upgrades highlighted in the PRAHAAR strategy.

## Economy & Finance
- **Tiger Conservation Policy Updated**: India's revised Tiger Conservation Policy mandated buffer zone development funds be shared with local communities — impacting forest-based livelihoods in AP's Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve.

## Science & Technology
- **ACASA-India Atlas**: Ministry of Agriculture launched the **Atlas of Climate Adaptation in Indian Agriculture (ACASA-India)** — a district-wise mapping of climate risks and crop-specific adaptation strategies for farmers.
