# Current Affairs — Week of May 11, 2026

## International
- **India's Diplomatic Engagement**: India's Foreign Minister attended G7 Foreign Ministers' Meeting as an invited partner — focused on Ukraine resolution, Indo-Pacific security, and AI governance frameworks.
- **Global Trade: India-EU FTA Progress**: India-EU FTA moved to final negotiating round with tariff schedules under review; expected finalisation by end of 2026 — India's largest potential FTA partner ($140 billion trade).

## National
- **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool (BMIP) Launched (May 12)**: India launched the **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool** in New Delhi — a government-backed pool to insure Indian-flagged vessels against war risks, reducing dependence on foreign P&I clubs.
- **National Florence Nightingale Awards (May 12)**: 15 nursing professionals conferred National Florence Nightingale Awards at Rashtrapati Bhavan on International Nurses Day — highest national recognition for nursing excellence.
- **Ahmedabad-Dholera Rail Line (May 13)**: Union Cabinet approved the ₹20,667 crore semi high-speed double-line project (134 km) connecting Ahmedabad (Sarkhej) to Dholera SIR — enabling the greenfield smart city's growth.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Chandrababu's Investment Drive**: AP CM Naidu's Singapore roadshow resulted in MoUs worth ₹45,000 crore in data centre, semiconductor, and logistics sectors — to be formalised at the upcoming AP Global Investors Summit.
- **AP Nursing Excellence**: 3 nurses from AP government hospitals were among the 15 National Florence Nightingale Award 2026 winners — highlighting AP's public health infrastructure investments.
- **Polavaram Monsoon Readiness**: AP government completed pre-monsoon safety checks on Polavaram dam; irrigation water expected to reach 16 lakh acres of Godavari delta farmland by Kharif 2026.

## Economy & Finance
- **BMIP — Strategic Significance**: Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool protects Indian shipping from war-risk premium surges (as seen in Red Sea crisis 2024); supports India's goal of 5% global shipping share by 2030.
- **India Inflation**: CPI inflation eased to 3.4% in April 2026 — below RBI's 4% target for the second consecutive month; supporting case for a rate cut at June MPC meeting.

## Science & Technology
- **ISRO PSLV-C65 Preparations**: ISRO announced PSLV-C65 mission for June 2026 carrying the NaVIC-3 navigation satellite — further strengthening India's indigenous GPS-equivalent constellation.
