# Current Affairs — Week of November 3, 2025

## International
- **PM E-DRIVE Scheme Crosses ₹1,634 Crore**: PM E-DRIVE (Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement) disbursed **₹1,634.62 crore** in EV subsidies till November 23, 2025 — covering 2-wheelers, 3-wheelers, and state-run e-buses under FAME III successor.
- **India-ASEAN Digital MoU Signed**: India and ASEAN formally signed the Digital Economy Cooperation Framework — covering data flows, cybersecurity, fintech, and AI governance; ASEAN is India's 4th largest trading partner.

## National
- **Bihar Elections Phase 1 (Nov 6)**: Phase 1 of Bihar Assembly elections held on November 6 — polling in 121 constituencies; voter turnout reported at 62.3%; exit polls predicted NDA sweep.
- **Bihar Elections Phase 2 (Nov 11)**: Phase 2 voting in remaining 122 constituencies on November 11; overall Bihar turnout 61.8% — lower than 2020 (57.05%); counting scheduled November 14.
- **Bharat Taxi Launched in Delhi**: India's first cooperative cab service **Bharat Taxi** launched in Delhi with 650 driver-owners on November 3; onboarded through ONDC; fares 15% lower than app-based aggregators.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP Quantum Computer Installation**: AP's Amaravati campus began installation of a **133-qubit quantum computer** (IBM partnership with TCS and L&T) — first commercial quantum facility in a state capital; supports AI and biotech research.
- **AP EV Adoption**: AP government's EV policy offered subsidies under PM E-DRIVE for 3-wheelers; Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam emerged as top EV auto-rickshaw adopting cities in AP.

## Economy & Finance
- **EV Subsidy under PM E-DRIVE**: ₹1,634 crore disbursed for ~4 lakh EVs; 2-wheelers dominate (70% of units); scheme runs until 2027; targets 10 lakh EV subsidy beneficiaries.

## Science & Technology
- **Magnetic Nanobots for Cancer**: IISc Bengaluru's Dr. Ambarish Ghosh won the **2025 New York Academy of Sciences–Tata Sons Transformation Prize** for magnetic nanobot research — targeted cancer therapy delivering drugs directly to tumour sites using magnetic fields.
