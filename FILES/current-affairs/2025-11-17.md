# Current Affairs — Week of November 17, 2025

## International
- **India-G20 Sherpa Meeting**: India participated in G20 Sherpa-level meetings in Johannesburg (South Africa G20 presidency); focused on debt relief for Global South, AI governance, and climate finance.
- **India-UK FTA Nearing Completion**: UK PM and India PM spoke on the sidelines of G20 preparatory events; FTA's last hurdles — whisky tariffs and student visa reciprocity — under final negotiation.

## National
- **Nitish Kumar Sworn In Again — 10th Time (Nov 20)**: Nitish Kumar took oath as Bihar CM for a **record 10th time** on November 20 — a political arrangement within NDA to maintain Nitish's stature while BJP holds key cabinet posts.
- **Nowgam Police Station Blast (Nov 15)**: Nine people killed in an explosion at the Nowgam police station in Jammu & Kashmir — suspected IED; NIA-led probe; J&K police put on high alert.
- **Jan Aushadhi Network Reaches 17,610**: PM Jan Aushadhi Pariyojana now operates **17,610 Kendras** nationwide offering ~2,110 drugs and 315 surgical items at affordable prices — government announced target of 25,000 stores by 2026.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **AP National Energy Conservation Award (Advance Notice)**: AP government's energy department confirmed AP would receive the **National Energy Conservation Award** in December — marking the 4th consecutive year of recognition.
- **AP Aquaculture Hub**: AP government approved a ₹3,200 crore aquaculture modernisation plan — upgrading hatcheries, cold chain infrastructure, and processing plants in East Godavari and West Godavari districts.

## Economy & Finance
- **India Current Account Deficit**: India's CAD for Q2 FY2025-26 reported at 1.1% of GDP — improved from 1.4% in Q1; driven by strong services exports (IT, software) and lower oil import bill.

## Science & Technology
- **Aravalli Hills Conservation**: Supreme Court stayed its November 20 order on Aravalli hills — protecting 75,000 acres of ecologically sensitive Aravalli range from construction; significant for Delhi-NCR air quality and groundwater recharge.
