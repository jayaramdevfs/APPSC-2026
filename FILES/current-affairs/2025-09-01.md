# Current Affairs — Week of September 1, 2025

## International
- **India-Mauritius Special Economic Package**: PM Navinchandra Ramgoolam visited Varanasi; India announced a **USD 680 million Special Economic Package** for Mauritius covering health, infrastructure, maritime security, and education — deepening India-Mauritius ties.
- **Zapad 2025 Military Exercise**: An Indian Armed Forces contingent participated in the Russia-led multilateral exercise **Zapad 2025**, designed to simulate high-intensity conventional warfare and counter-terrorism operations.

## National
- **GST 2.0 Reform Discussions**: Centre initiated consultations on GST 2.0 — simplifying the 4-slab structure, reducing compliance costs, and removing the inverted duty structure affecting MSMEs and exporters.
- **SOAR Programme (Skilling for AI Readiness)**: Govt of India's SOAR programme — integrating AI education into Classes 6-12 curriculum — began pilot rollout in 12 states; targets 5 crore AI-literate students by 2028.
- **UNESCO Biosphere Reserve**: India's **Cold Desert Biosphere Reserve** (Himachal Pradesh) was accepted into UNESCO's World Network of Biosphere Reserves at the 37th ICC-MAB session — India's first high-altitude cold desert site in the global network.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **Amaravati Capital Works Restart**: AP government formally restarted ₹56,000 crore capital construction projects — High Court, Legislative Assembly, State Secretariat, and HoD office towers — with a 2028 completion target.
- **AP Investment Drive**: CM Chandrababu Naidu's government claimed ₹10 lakh crore ($120 billion) in investment MoUs secured in the first 16 months of TDP-led government, across IT, infrastructure, and manufacturing.

## Economy & Finance
- **GST Council Agenda**: Pre-GST 2.0 consultations focused on merging 12% and 18% slabs, rationalising exemptions for health and education, and introducing a simplified composition scheme for MSMEs.

## Science & Technology
- **India Cold Desert Biosphere**: Cold Desert BR covers Spiti Valley and Pin Valley (Himachal Pradesh) — high altitude trans-Himalayan ecosystem; significance for climate research and snow leopard conservation.
