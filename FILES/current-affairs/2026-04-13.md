# Current Affairs — Week of April 13, 2026

## International
- **India-Oman CEPA Finalised**: India and Oman finalised all chapters of the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA); expected to come into force June 1, 2026. Covers goods, services, investment, and digital trade.
- **WHO Global Health Report**: WHO released its annual global health security report ranking India 47th globally — up from 57th in 2023; citing improvements in pandemic preparedness infrastructure.

## National
- **Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) Rules Notified**: Central government notified the final UPS (Unified Pension Scheme) rules effective **April 1, 2026** — replacing NPS for new central government employees who opt in; guarantees 50% of last salary as pension after 25 years of service.
- **West Bengal Pre-Poll Tensions**: ECI deployed 600+ companies of central forces ahead of West Bengal elections; EC invoked Model Code of Conduct across the state.
- **ISRO EOS-09 Launch**: ISRO successfully launched **EOS-09** earth observation satellite via PSLV-C62 — enhances India's agricultural monitoring, disaster management, and urban planning capabilities.

## Andhra Pradesh ⭐
- **YSR Rythu Bharosa 10th Instalment**: AP government released the 10th instalment of YSR Rythu Bharosa (Direct Benefit Transfer for farmers) — ₹7,500 credited to farmer accounts covering over 50 lakh beneficiaries.
- **Amaravati Construction Phase 2**: CM Chandrababu Naidu inaugurated the Phase 1 Secretariat buildings in Amaravati — first functioning government offices in the new capital city.
- **AP Fisheries Export**: AP achieved ₹22,000 crore in fisheries exports for 2025-26 — largest fisheries exporting state; prawn/shrimp aquaculture from Godavari-Krishna belt dominates.

## Economy & Finance
- **UPS vs NPS**: Unified Pension Scheme guarantees defined benefit (50% last pay after 25 years); NPS was defined contribution with market-linked returns — UPS provides greater security but higher government liability.

## Science & Technology
- **EOS-09 Capabilities**: Earth observation satellite with 0.5-metre resolution — enables real-time crop health monitoring, flood mapping, and coastal erosion tracking; directly benefits AP's agricultural planning.

## APPSC Angle
> **What to remember for exam:**
> - YSR Rythu Bharosa: AP direct income support to farmers; ₹7,500 per year per farmer family
> - Unified Pension Scheme (UPS): Effective April 1, 2026; 50% of last pay guarantee; replaces NPS for eligible central govt employees
> - EOS-09: Earth observation; PSLV-C62; 0.5m resolution
